## 摘要
我们汇编了127个主星（另有6个候选体）的数据，并将其作为约束条件，利用MESA代码构建主星的内部模型。从这些模型中得出了两个重要结论。第一，除少数恒星外，主星的金属丰度（$Z_0$）大于0.007。这表明在$Z_0\approx0.007$以下，行星的形成可能受到抑制。第二，它涉及银盘化学演化的过程。对于给定的$Z_0$值，考虑到最古老的恒星，$Z_0$与年龄（$t_9$）之间存在线性关系。该直线在$Z_0=0$时对应于$t_9=13.4$ Gyr，这一数值与银河系的年龄一致。该线性关系持续到$t_9=6$ Gyr附近，且在$t_9=2-6$ Gyr之间，$Z_0$的最大值保持恒定。我们进一步对文献中归类为红团簇（RC）星的12个主星进行了建模，明确考虑了红巨星分支上的质量损失。这些模型凸显了质量损失假设在确定RC主星的初始质量和年龄方面的关键作用，及其对近距离行星存活和演化的影响。本研究的另一个关键成果是发现了主星的$Z_0$与观测金属丰度（$Z_{\rm s}$）之间的关系。我们获得了$Z_0$（模型的输入参数）作为恒星质量、半径和$Z_{\rm s}$的函数的有用表达式。该表达式可用于根据微观扩散导致的表面金属丰度降低来估算$Z_0$。我们还推导了行星质量与轨道半长轴及主星质量的关系式。该表达式可能指示这些行星形成于内盘附近的质量分布，热木星除外。行星半径似乎取决于行星质量、辐照能量以及轨道周期。

## Abstract
We compiled data for 127 hosts (plus six candidates) and used them as constraints to construct interior models of the hosts using the {\small MESA} code. Two significant conclusions emerge from these models. First, except for a few stars, the hosts' metallicity ($Z_0$) is greater than 0.007. This suggests a possible suppression of the occurrence of planets below $Z_0\approx0.007$. Second, it concerns how chemical evolution unfolds in the galactic disc. For a given $Z_0$ value, considering the oldest stars, there is a linear relationship between $Z_0$ and age ($t_9$). This line is around $t_9=13.4$ Gyr at $Z_0=0$, a value consistent with the age of the Galaxy. The linear relationship continues until around $t_9=6$ Gyr, and the maximum value of $Z_0$ remains constant between $t_9=2-6$ Gyr. We further modelled 12 hosts classified as red clump (RC) stars in the literature, explicitly accounting for mass loss along the red giant branch. These models highlight the critical role of mass-loss assumptions in determining the initial masses and ages of RC hosts, and their implications for the survival and evolution of close-in planets. Another key outcome of this study is the discovery of the relationship between $Z_0$ and the observed metallicity ($Z_{\rm s}$) for the hosts. We obtain a useful expression for $Z_0$, the input parameter for the models, as a function of stellar mass, radius, and $Z_{\rm s}$. This expression can be used to estimate $Z_0$ based on the reduced surface metallicity due to microscopic diffusion. We also derive an expression for planetary mass relative to the orbital semimajor axis and host mass. This expression may indicate a mass distribution near the inner disc where these planets formed, except for hot-Jupiters. Planet radii appear to depend on the planet's mass and irradiation energy, as well as the orbital period.
