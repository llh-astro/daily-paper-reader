## 摘要
搜寻恒星宜居带中的地球大小行星，归根结底是在嘈杂的光变曲线中寻找数量极少、深度极浅且间隔很远的凌星信号。对于类太阳恒星，宜居带行星通常具有很长的轨道周期，因此有限的观测基线可能只能记录到少数几次凌星。同时，恒星自身的活动变化、仪器系统效应以及数据缺口都可能掩盖这些微弱事件，或产生类似凌星的亮度变化。本文综述了人工智能辅助光变曲线预处理、弱信号搜索、候选体核查和参数推断方面的最新进展，并探讨了预训练和多模态模型的潜在作用。综合来看，所综述的研究表明，人工智能在实际应用中能够提高大规模光变曲线分析的效率，在某些注入测试中能恢复更多低信噪比信号，并减少需要人工复核的目标数量。本文认为，将人工智能与物理建模、统计推断和后续观测相结合，为提高宜居带候选体研究的发现效率和可靠性提供了一条切实可行的途径。

## Abstract
Searching for Earth-sized planets in stellar habitable zones ultimately means finding a small number of very shallow, widely separated transits in noisy light curves. Around Sun-like stars, habitable-zone planets generally have long orbital periods, so a finite observing baseline may record only a few transits. At the same time, intrinsic stellar variability, instrumental systematics, and data gaps can obscure these weak events or produce transit-like brightness changes. This review examines recent progress in AI-assisted light-curve preprocessing, weak-signal searches, candidate vetting, and parameter inference, and considers the potential roles of pretraining and multimodal models. Taken together, the studies reviewed here show that AI has practical value in making large-scale light-curve analysis more efficient, recovering more signals at low signal-to-noise ratios in some injection tests, and reducing the number of targets requiring manual review. This review concludes that combining AI with physical modeling, statistical inference, and follow-up observations provides a practical route to improve both the discovery efficiency and the reliability of habitable-zone candidate studies.
