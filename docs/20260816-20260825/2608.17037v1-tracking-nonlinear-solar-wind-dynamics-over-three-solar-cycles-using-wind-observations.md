## 摘要
太阳风是一种湍流、弱碰撞等离子体，其特征包括非高斯涨落、长程相关性和多重分形动力学。我们利用Wind航天器在1995年至2025年间直接获得的每小时质子密度测量数据，研究这些特性的长期演变。使用单航天器数据集对先前基于多航天器OMNI数据库得出的结果进行独立检验。在按月移动的一年滑动窗口内估计非广延$q$-三重态的三个分量：$q_{stat}$来自密度增量的分布，$q_{rel}$来自自相关函数的衰减，$q_{sens}$来自多重分形谱。它们的平均值$q_{stat}=1.71\pm0.07$，$q_{rel}=4.62\pm0.52$，$q_{sens}=-0.45\pm0.27$，证实了重尾统计、慢弛豫和弱混沌多重分形动力学的持续存在。然而，这些参数表现出明显的时间变化性以及与太阳活动的关系。$q_{stat}$的傅里叶谱包含一个约10.1年的主导周期，而它与太阳黑子数的相关性为中等正相关（$r=0.611$）。相关性和互信息分析表明，$q_{stat}$主要与太阳代理指标相关，而$q_{sens}$与地磁指数表现出更强的关系，$q_{rel}$则表现出较弱的依赖性。约2004年周围$q_{stat}$的异常增强恰逢一系列强烈的行星际扰动，尽管也必须考虑与Wind轨道变化相关的可能影响。这些发现证明了非广延描述的稳健性，并表明太阳风质子密度的统计和动力学特性展现出受太阳活动调制的证据。

## Abstract
The solar wind is a turbulent, weakly collisional plasma characterized by non-Gaussian fluctuations, long-range correlations, and multifractal dynamics. We investigate the long-term evolution of these properties using hourly proton density measurements obtained directly by the Wind spacecraft over 1995--2025. The use of a single-spacecraft dataset provides an independent test of previous results derived from the multi-spacecraft OMNI database. The three components of the nonextensive $q$-triplet are estimated within one-year sliding windows shifted monthly: $q_{stat}$ from the distribution of density increments, $q_{rel}$ from the decay of the autocorrelation function, and $q_{sens}$ from the multifractal spectrum. Their mean values, $q_{stat}=1.71\pm0.07$, $q_{rel}=4.62\pm0.52$, and $q_{sens}=-0.45\pm0.27$, confirm the persistent presence of heavy-tailed statistics, slow relaxation, and weakly chaotic multifractal dynamics. The parameters nevertheless exhibit distinct temporal variability and relationships with solar activity. The Fourier spectrum of $q_{stat}$ contains a dominant period of approximately $10.1$ years, while its correlation with the sunspot number is positive and moderate ($r=0.611$). Correlation and mutual-information analyses show that $q_{stat}$ is primarily associated with solar proxies, whereas $q_{sens}$ displays stronger relationships with geomagnetic indices and $q_{rel}$ exhibits weaker dependencies. An anomalous enhancement of $q_{stat}$ around 2004 coincides with a sequence of intense interplanetary disturbances, although possible effects related to Wind's orbital transition must also be considered. These findings demonstrate the robustness of the nonextensive description and show that the statistical and dynamical properties of solar wind proton density show evidence of modulation by solar activity.
