## 摘要
逆行S型行星已在多个双星系统中被观测到，但其形成路径仍知之甚少。通过高分辨率流体动力学模拟，我们证明了一个围绕偏心、不等质量双星的极向双星盘能够形成并维持主星周围的逆行小盘。这为逆行S型行星提供了一条直接的原位形成通道。小盘通过一个与极向盘略微错位的亚开普勒吸积流形成。小盘最初经历von Zeipel-Kozai-Lidov（ZKL）振荡，驱动耦合的偏心率和倾角演化。内部小盘并非无限振荡，而是演化越过临界ZKL倾角，与外盘解耦，并稳定在逆行轨道上。这种演化对数值分辨率敏感：在之前的较低分辨率模拟中，逆行构型缺失，因为小盘吸积时标太短，无法维持ZKL驱动的演化。对于较高的盘黏度，由于吸积时标较短，小盘保持近极向。由于原行星盘通常具有低黏度，我们的结果表明，逆行S型行星可以从极向双星盘周围的逆行小盘中原位形成，且其发生频率可能高于当前估计。

## Abstract
Retrograde S-type planets have been observed in several binary systems, yet their formation pathway remains poorly understood. With high-resolution hydrodynamic simulations, we demonstrate that a polar circumbinary disk around an eccentric, unequal-mass binary can form and sustain a retrograde mini disk around the primary star. This provides a direct in-situ formation channel for retrograde S-type planets. The mini disk forms via a sub-Keplerian accretion stream that is slightly misaligned from the polar disk. The mini disk initially undergoes von Zeipel-Kozai-Lidov (ZKL) oscillations, driving coupled eccentricity and inclination evolution. Rather than oscillating indefinitely, the inner mini disk evolves past the critical ZKL inclination, decouples from the outer disk, and settles into a stable retrograde orbit. This evolution is sensitive to numerical resolution: the retrograde configuration is absent in previous lower-resolution simulations, where the mini disk accretion timescale is too short to sustain ZKL-driven evolution. For a higher disk viscosity, the mini disk remains near-polar due to a shorter accretion timescale. Since protoplanetary disks typically have low viscosity, our results suggest that retrograde S-type planets can form in-situ from retrograde mini disks around polar circumbinary disks, and their occurrence rate may be higher than currently estimated.
