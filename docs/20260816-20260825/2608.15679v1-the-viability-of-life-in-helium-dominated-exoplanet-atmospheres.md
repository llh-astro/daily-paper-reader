## 摘要
氦是宇宙中第二丰富的元素，但氦主导的大气却很少被视为生命的环境。有两个假设导致了这种忽视：氦预计会与氢一起从岩石行星上逃逸，而地球本身也缺乏厚实的氦包层。最近的观测和大气演化模型表明，岩石系外行星可以保留氦主导的大气。在此，我们通过综合一批很大程度上被忽视的关于氦主导大气中生命的实验室研究，为在这种环境中的生命提供论据。所有被研究的生物，包括细菌、真菌、原生动物、微藻、植物和动物，在提供基本代谢需求的情况下都能耐受氦主导的大气。在一系列压力下，氦未显示出毒性，也未对代谢、细胞分裂、光合作用、固氮或协调的多细胞活动（包括人类）构成根本障碍。因此，氦主导的大气是生命可行的且未被充分认识的行星环境。氦是化学惰性的，光谱上不显眼，因此主体大气既不会促成生物标志气体的化学破坏，也不会强烈遮蔽其光谱特征。氦主导的大气还具有低平均分子量和大标高，增强了透射光谱特征并改善了远程可探测性。

## Abstract
Helium is the second most abundant element in the Universe, yet helium-dominated atmospheres are rarely considered as environments for life. Two assumptions have contributed to this neglect: helium is expected to escape from rocky planets together with hydrogen, and Earth itself lacks a substantial helium envelope. Recent observations and atmospheric evolution models, indicate that rocky exoplanets can retain helium-dominated atmospheres. Here we make the case for life in such environments by synthesizing a largely overlooked body of laboratory research on life in helium-dominated atmospheres. Every organism studied, including bacteria, fungi, protozoa, microalgae, plants, and animals tolerates helium-dominated atmospheres when supplied with essential metabolic requirements. Across a range of pressures, helium has shown no demonstrated toxicity and no fundamental barrier to metabolism, cell division, photosynthesis, nitrogen fixation, or coordinated multicellular activity, including in humans. Helium-dominated atmospheres therefore constitute a viable and underrecognized planetary environment for life. Helium is chemically inert and spectroscopically unobtrusive, so the bulk atmosphere would neither contribute to the chemical destruction of biosignature gases nor strongly obscure their spectral features. Helium-dominated atmospheres also have low mean molecular weights and large scale heights, strengthening spectral features in transmission and improving remote detectability.
