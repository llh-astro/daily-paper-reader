## 摘要
JWST使得能够测量直接成像行星大气中的碳、氧和硫丰度。从行星形成角度解读这些丰度需要对应宿主星的丰度。在本工作中，我们利用高分辨率双子座/GHOST光谱对冷成像行星宿主AF Lep A和$ε$ Indi A进行了详细表征。我们使用两种不同方法推导了大气参数$T_{\rm eff}$和$\log{g}$，显示出$T_{\rm eff}$存在高达约260 K的差异。随后将推导出的参数用于测量16种元素丰度（C、O、Na、Mg、Si、S、K、Ca、Sc、Ti、Cr、Mn、Fe、Ni、Zn、Y）及若干丰度比。利用光谱拟合和等效宽度两种方法，我们发现AF Lep A和$ε$ Indi A的太阳C/O、C/S和O/S比值（<1.5σ）。我们将测得的丰度及其比值与行星AF Lep b和$ε$ Indi Ab的丰度进行比较，行星相对于其宿主星的丰度升高强烈表明其通过核吸积路径形成。

## Abstract
JWST has enabled the measurement of carbon, oxygen, and sulfur abundances in the atmospheres of directly imaged planets. Interpretation of these abundances from a planet formation standpoint requires the corresponding abundances for the host star. In this work, we present detailed characterizations of the cold imaged planet hosts AF Lep A and $ε$ Indi A using high-resolution Gemini/GHOST spectra. We derive the atmospheric parameters $T_{\rm eff}$ and $\log{g}$ using two different approaches, revealing differences in $T_{\rm eff}$ up to $\sim260\,$K. The derived parameters are subsequently incorporated in measurement of 16 elemental abundances (C, O, Na, Mg, Si, S, K, Ca, Sc, Ti, Cr, Mn, Fe, Ni, Zn, Y) and several abundance ratios. Utilizing both the spectral fit and the equivalent width methods, we find solar C/O, C/S and O/S ratios ($<1.5σ$) for AF Lep A and $ε$ Indi A. We compare our measured abundances and their ratios with those of the planets AF Lep b and $ε$ Indi Ab, with the elevated abundances for the planets relative to their host stars strongly indicating formation by core-accretion pathways.
