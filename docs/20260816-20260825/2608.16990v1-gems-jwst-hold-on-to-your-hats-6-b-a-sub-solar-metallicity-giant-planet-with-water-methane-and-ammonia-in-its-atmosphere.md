## 摘要
HATS-6 b是近期发现的几颗围绕M矮星运行的巨系外行星（GEMS）之一，也是JWST巡天的一部分，该巡天旨在将这些稀有行星的整体和大气性质与FGK恒星对应的行星进行比较。HATS-6 b是一颗温暖的（$\mathrm{T_{eq}}\sim700$ K）、土星质量（$M_p\sim0.3~\mathrm{M_J}$）、木星半径（$R_p\sim1~\mathrm{R_J}$）的行星，每约3天凌星一次。在本研究中，我们展示了利用JWST近红外光谱仪（NIRSpec）的PRISM模式，通过两次凌星获得的HATS-6 b透射光谱，波长覆盖范围为$0.6-5.3$微米。我们使用正向建模与自由化学反演之间的迭代方法分析这些JWST观测数据，得出低金属丰度（$\log\mathrm{[M/H]}=-1.99^{+0.2}_{-0.2}$）和亚太阳C/O比（$\log\mathrm{[C/O]=-0.46^{+0.2}_{-0.2}}$）的大气，并发现H$_2$O、CH$_4$和NH$_3$的强证据，其体积混合比（以$\log[X]$表示）分别为$-4.88_{-0.24}^{+0.25}$、$-5.38_{-0.19}^{+0.18}$和$-6.03_{-0.19}^{+0.18}$。我们一致地反演出比HATS-6 b轨道构型预测的$\mathrm{T_{eq}}$显著更低的值，这一结果不受任何数据缩减和反演选择的影响，表明存在非零键反照率。我们的行星内部模型反演出的大体金属丰度比我们反演出的大气金属丰度高三个数量级，这也表明大气并未充分混合。我们在约3微米处发现了一个多余特征，并扩展了对此的可能解释，例如存在HCN或碳氢化合物如C$_2$H$_4$。然而，由于该波长区域碳氢化合物特征存在简并性，我们不对该多余特征下结论，而是鼓励对这个有趣目标进行进一步观测和后续研究。

## Abstract
HATS-6 b is one of several recently discovered Giant Exoplanets orbiting M-dwarf Stars (GEMS) and is part of a JWST survey that aims to compare bulk and atmospheric properties of these rare planets against their FGK star counterparts. HATS-6 b is a warm ($\mathrm{T_{eq}}\sim700$ K), Saturn-mass ($M_p\sim0.3~\mathrm{M_J}$), Jupiter-radius ($R_p\sim1~\mathrm{R_J}$) planet that transits its star every $\sim$ 3 days. In this study, we present the transmission spectrum of HATS-6 b obtained with two transits using the PRISM mode of JWST Near Infrared Spectrograph (NIRSpec), spanning a wavelength range of $0.6-5.3$ um. Analyzing these JWST observations using an iterative approach between forward modeling and free chemistry retrievals, we derive a low metallicity ($\log\mathrm{[M/H]}=-1.99^{+0.2}_{-0.2}$) sub-solar C/O ($\log\mathrm{[C/O]=-0.46^{+0.2}_{-0.2}}$) atmosphere, and find strong evidence for H$_2$O, CH$_4$, and NH$_3$ at volume mixing ratios (in $\log[X]$) of $-4.88_{-0.24}^{+0.25}$, $-5.38_{-0.19}^{+0.18}$, and $-6.03_{-0.19}^{+0.18}$, respectively. We consistently retrieve a significantly lower $\mathrm{T_{eq}}$ than predicted from the orbital configuration of HATS-6 b, which was impervious to any data reduction and retrieval choices, suggesting a non-zero bond albedo. Our planetary interior models retrieve bulk metallicities three orders of magnitude larger than our retrieved atmospheric metallicity, also suggesting that the atmosphere is not well-mixed. We find an excess feature around 3 um, and expand on possible explanations for this, such as the presence of HCN or hydrocarbons like C$_2$H$_4$. Yet, due to the degeneracies present for hydrocarbon features in this wavelength region, we do not draw any conclusions about the excess feature and instead encourage further observations and follow-up of this intriguing target.
