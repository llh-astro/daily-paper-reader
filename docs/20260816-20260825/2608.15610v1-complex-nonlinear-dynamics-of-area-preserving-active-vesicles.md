## 摘要
我们研究了低雷诺数下具有局部不可延展膜的主动驱动准球形囊泡的非线性形状动力学和自主推进。从Stokes水动力学、线性化膜弹性和谐波主动强迫出发，我们推导出球谐形变模态的约化描述。由局部不可延展性实施的全局面积约束是动态非线性的唯一来源。它将动力学限制在可能形状空间中的紧致流形上。自主推进通过非线性模态耦合产生，并由形状空间中轨迹扫过的有向面积几何决定。对于两种主动模态，动力学简化为周期驱动的相位方程，表现出同步、相位滑移和模式锁定。引入第三种主动模态从根本上改变动力学，产生准周期不变环面和共振周期循环。复发诊断揭示由此产生的共振结构，而循环平均推进的涨落为潜在的形状动力学提供了实验可及的特征。我们的结果表明，对于主动驱动的囊泡，一个几何约束足以将原本线性的动力系统转变为具有丰富非线性动力学的系统。

## Abstract
We investigate the nonlinear shape dynamics and autonomous propulsion of actively driven quasi-spherical vesicles with locally inextensible membranes at low Reynolds number. Starting from Stokes hydrodynamics, linearized membrane elasticity, and harmonic active forcing, we derive a reduced description in terms of spherical harmonic deformation modes. The global area constraint enforced by local inextensibility is the sole source of dynamic nonlinearity. It confines the dynamics to compact manifolds in the space of possible shapes. Autonomous propulsion arises through nonlinear mode coupling and is determined geometrically by the oriented area swept by the trajectories in shape space. For two active modes, the dynamics reduces to a periodically driven phase equation exhibiting synchronization, phase slips, and mode locking. Introducing a third active mode fundamentally changes the dynamics, giving rise to quasiperiodic invariant tori and resonant periodic cycles. A recurrence diagnostic reveals the resulting resonance structure, while fluctuations of the cycle-averaged propulsion provide an experimentally accessible signature of the underlying shape dynamics. Our results demonstrate that, for actively driven vesicles, a geometric constraint is sufficient to transform an otherwise linear dynamical system into one exhibiting rich nonlinear dynamics.
