## 摘要
本文在有限维交换实代数中构建了动力系统的符号表示。该代数框架（正交幂等元、零因子以及有限阶代数的结构）支持代数值傅里叶变换与拉普拉斯变换，以及基于沃尔什函数的系统辨识。这些方法为在非惯性参考系中的运动给出了精确的、无坐标的矢量解，包括广义拉莫尔定理、傅科摆、中心位置力场中的运动，以及任意旋转参考系中的二体问题。本英文版是对 1995 年原版的经审核的 LaTeX 重建，并附有一份记录其来源的编者说明。

## Abstract
The thesis develops symbolic representations of dynamical systems in finite-dimensional commutative real algebras. The algebraic framework (orthogonal idempotents, zero divisors, and the structure of finite-order algebras) supports algebra-valued Fourier and Laplace transforms and Walsh-function-based system identification. The methods yield exact, coordinate-free vectorial solutions for motion in non-inertial reference frames, including a generalized Larmor theorem, the Foucault pendulum, motion in central positional-force fields, and the two-body problem in arbitrarily rotating reference frames. This English edition is an audited LaTeX reconstruction of the 1995 original, with an editorial note documenting provenance.
