## 摘要
我们研究了仅存在于恒星黑子中的一条代表性单光谱线如何在行星高分辨率吸收光谱中引入畸变。具体来说，我们评估了这类黑子诱导的特征是否可能类似于行星大气吸收，以及在何种观测或恒星条件下它们变得显著。利用SOAPv4代码，我们模拟了一颗无大气行星凌越一颗黑子含有额外吸收线（相对于光球光谱）的恒星时的凌星观测。我们模拟了两个系统：一颗近距离的木星大小行星和一颗遥远的类地球大小的行星，均凌越太阳大小的恒星。我们改变了恒星自转周期和黑子大小，并确保行星凌越黑子。光谱线被理想化为高斯线型以隔离黑子特定特征的影响。我们表明，单条恒星黑子线可以在吸收光谱中产生伪特征。这些特征污染吸收光谱的方式如果不加以考虑可能导致误读。对于具有短凌星持续时间的木星大小行星，这些特征主要表现为发射状，但仍可能抑制潜在的真实大气吸收信号。对于具有长凌星持续时间的类地球大小行星，畸变主要表现为吸收状，并可能被解释为行星吸收。这些结果突显了在高分辨率吸收光谱分析中考虑恒星不均匀性和活动的重要性。

## Abstract
We investigate how a representative single spectral line present only in a stellar spot can introduce distortions in high-resolution planetary absorption spectra. Specifically, we assess whether such spot-induced features may resemble planetary atmospheric absorption and the observational or stellar conditions under which they become significant. Using the SOAPv4 code, we simulated transit observations of an atmosphere-less planet across a star with a spot containing an additional absorption line compared to the photosphere's spectrum. Two systems were modelled: a close-in Jupiter-sized planet and a distant Earth-sized planet transiting a Sun-sized star. We varied the stellar rotation period and the spot size, and ensured that the planet transits the spot. Spectral lines were idealised Gaussians to isolate the impact of the spot-specific feature. We show that a single stellar spot line can generate spurious features in the absorption spectrum. These features contaminate the absorption spectrum in ways that may lead to misinterpretation if not accounted for. For Jupiter-sized planets with short transits, the features are mainly emission-like, but can nonetheless dampen a potential true atmospheric absorption signature. For Earth-sized planets with long transits, the distortion is mainly absorption-like and could be interpreted as planetary absorption. These results highlight the importance of accounting for stellar heterogeneity and activity in the analysis of high-resolution absorption spectra.
