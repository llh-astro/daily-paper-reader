## 摘要
TRAPPIST-1系统因其七颗凌日地球大小的系外行星而闻名。该系统已被广泛研究和表征，特别是通过凌日时间变化（TTVs）来精确测量行星的质量。利用SPIRou Legacy Survey和NIRPS保证时间观测计划获得的近红外光谱观测数据，我们旨在通过系统的视向速度（RV）测量来验证这些数值。我们的RV分析表明，当前数据尚不具备单独探测TRAPPIST-1行星所需的精度。然而，我们确信地探测到了行星联合RV信号（$\Delta\ln\mathcal{Z}=7.53$，优势比1860:1），通过将其相对质量约束于TTV分析，并以TRAPPIST-1 b作为整个系统的代表。首次恢复了TRAPPIST-1系统的RV信号：我们发现RV半振幅为$K_b=3.65^{+0.78}_{-0.83}$ m s$^{-1}$，对应的行星质量为$M_{p,\,b}=1.31\pm0.29$ M$_\oplus$，表明RV测量与TTV模型（$M_{p,\,b;\,\text{TTV}}=1.374\pm0.069$ M$_\oplus$）一致。此外，NIRPS的RV数据约束了雪线之外巨行星的存在，排除了轨道周期达2.7年的土星质量行星和轨道周期达20天的海王星质量天体。通过RV，我们确定恒星活动周期为$3.22^{+0.22}_{-0.20}$天。其与光度测量（K2和TESS）的一致性证实了恒星自转是TRAPPIST-1观测到的$\sim3.3$天周期性的起源。我们进一步利用SPIRou偏振测量研究了恒星活动，对纵向磁场设置了一个上限（$|B_l|<40$ G，$3σ$）。该上限与弱多极大尺度磁场几何结构相容，正如在一些后期快速自转的M矮星中所观察到的那样。

## Abstract
The TRAPPIST-1 system is well-known for its seven transiting Earth-sized exoplanets. It has been extensively studied and characterized, notably with transit timing variations (TTVs) to precisely measure the mass of the planets. Using near-infrared spectroscopic observations obtained as part of the SPIRou Legacy Survey and the NIRPS Guaranteed Time Observation programs, we aimed to verify those values through radial velocity (RV) measurements of the system. Our RV analysis reveals that the current data do not have the precision required to individually detect the TRAPPIST-1 planets. However, we confidently detect ($Δ\ln\mathcal{Z}=7.53$, 1860:1 odds) the combined RV signature of the planets by informing their relative masses on the TTV analysis, with TRAPPIST-1 b as a proxy of the whole system. For the first time, the RV signal of the TRAPPIST-1 system is recovered: we find a RV semi-amplitude of $K_b=3.65^{+0.78}_{-0.83}$ m s$^{-1}$ corresponding to a planetary mass of $M_{p,\,b}=1.31\pm0.29$ M$_\oplus$, demonstrating that the RV measurements are consistent with the TTV model ($M_{p,\,b;\,\text{TTV}}=1.374\pm0.069$ M$_\oplus$). Additionally, the NIRPS RVs constrain the presence of giant planets beyond the snow line, excluding Saturn-mass planets out to 2.7-yr orbits and Neptune-mass objects out to 20 d. Through RV, we determined the stellar activity period to be of $3.22^{+0.22}_{-0.20}$ d. Its agreement with photometric measurements (K2 and TESS) confirms stellar rotation as the origin of the $\sim3.3$-d periodicity observed for TRAPPIST-1. We further investigated stellar activity with SPIRou polarimetric measurements, placing an upper limit on the longitudinal field ($|B_l|<40$ G, $3σ$). This limit is compatible with a weak multipolar large-scale magnetic geometry, as observed in some of the later-type rapidly rotating M dwarfs.
