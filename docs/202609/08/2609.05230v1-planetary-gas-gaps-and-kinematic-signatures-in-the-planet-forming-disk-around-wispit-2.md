## 摘要
围绕年轻恒星的尘埃气体盘中的巨行星形成过程在观测上仍缺乏约束。当前设施对盘内原行星的直接探测仍然有限。在没有探测到行星的情况下，通过次结构或偏离开普勒旋转的气体运动学特征来间接观测原行星的证据仍不明确。缺乏对次结构及其对应行星的联合探测，使得将行星形成过程与宿主环境联系起来具有挑战性。阿塔卡马大型毫米阵列（ALMA）的观测揭示了WISPIT 2原行星盘中气体间隙和运动学特征的清晰探测，这些特征与先前探测到的巨原行星的位置空间一致。新识别的气体间隙在12CO积分强度图和旋转曲线中均被观测到，与先前在散射光中识别的间隙相对应。它们的形态被证明与偏心间隙一致，正如巨行星-盘相互作用模型所预期的那样。外盘缺乏偏心性可以通过系统中存在第三颗行星来解释。WISPIT 2的观测提供了运动学特征、气体间隙与盘中巨行星形成之间长期寻求的经验桥梁。

## Abstract
The giant planet formation process in disks of gas and dust surrounding young stars is observationally still poorly constrained. Direct detection of protoplanets within the disk remains limited with current facilities. Indirect observational evidence of protoplanets through substructures or deviations from Keplerian rotation in the gas remains ambiguous in absence of detected planets. The lack of the combined detection of substructures and their corresponding planets makes it challenging to connect the planet formation process to the host environment. Observations with the Atacama Large Millimeter Array (ALMA) reveal clear detections of gas gaps and kinematic signatures in the WISPIT 2 protoplanetary disk that are cospatial with the locations of previously detected giant protoplanets. The newly identified gas gaps, observed in both the 12CO integrated intensity map and its rotation curve, correspond with the previously identified gaps in scattered light. Their morphologies are shown to be consistent with eccentric gaps, as expected from planet-disk interaction models of massive planets. The lack of eccentricity in the outer disk can be explained if a third planet is present in the system. The observations of WISPIT 2 provide a long-sought empirical bridge between kinematic signatures, gas gaps and giant planet formation in disks.
