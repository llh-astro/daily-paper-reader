## 摘要
恒星元素丰度为行星形成的化学环境提供了重要约束。M型和晚K型矮星是表征小型系外行星的主要目标，但其复杂光谱使得精确的元素丰度测量颇具挑战。在本工作中，我们提出了一个基于光谱合成的框架，用于从高分辨率近红外光谱中推导冷恒星的详细丰度。我们将该框架应用于五颗行星宿主恒星——GJ 9827、K2-18、TOI-1201、TOI-1685和巴纳德星——的SPIRou YJHK光谱，并为所有五颗恒星推导了C、O、Na、Mg、Al、Si、K、Ca、Ti、Cr、Mn和Fe的丰度，另外还对其中一部分恒星推导了Sc、V和Ni的丰度，典型不确定度为 $\lesssim 0.1$ dex。我们恢复的金属丰度和元素丰度与这些恒星的预期趋势以及先前文献分析大体一致。我们推导了C/O、Mg/Si、Fe/Mg和Fe/O的恒星比值，发现除贫金属的巴纳德星（[M/H] $= -0.33 \pm 0.06$ dex）外，所有五个系统均与太阳比值大体一致；巴纳德星明显贫铁（[Fe/H] $= -0.722 \pm 0.085$ dex）。我们的结果为利用哈勃空间望远镜（HST）和詹姆斯·韦布空间望远镜（JWST）对这些行星系统进行内部结构和大气研究建立了恒星化学基线，同时凸显了冷恒星精确丰度分析中仍然存在的模型依赖局限性。

## Abstract
Stellar elemental abundances provide important constraints on the chemical environments from which planets form. M and late-K dwarfs are prime targets for the characterization of small exoplanets, yet their complex spectra make accurate elemental abundance measurements challenging. In this work, we present a spectral synthesis-based framework to derive detailed abundances for cool stars from high-resolution, near-infrared spectra. We apply our framework to SPIRou YJHK spectra of five planet-host stars--GJ 9827, K2-18, TOI-1201, TOI-1685, and Barnard's Star--and derive abundances of C, O, Na, Mg, Al, Si, K, Ca, Ti, Cr, Mn, and Fe for all five stars, plus Sc, V, and Ni for a subset, with typical uncertainties of $\lesssim 0.1$ dex. Our recovered metallicities and elemental abundances are broadly consistent with expected trends and previous literature analyses of these stars. We derive stellar ratios of C/O, Mg/Si, Fe/Mg, and Fe/O and find that all five systems are broadly consistent with solar ratios, with the exception of the metal-poor Barnard's Star ([M/H] $= -0.33 \pm 0.06$ dex), which is substantially iron-poor ([Fe/H] $= -0.722 \pm 0.085$ dex). Our results establish stellar chemical baselines for interior structure and atmospheric studies of these planetary systems with the Hubble Space Telescope (HST) and James Webb Space Telescope (JWST), while highlighting the remaining model-dependent limitations of precise abundance analyses for cool stars.
