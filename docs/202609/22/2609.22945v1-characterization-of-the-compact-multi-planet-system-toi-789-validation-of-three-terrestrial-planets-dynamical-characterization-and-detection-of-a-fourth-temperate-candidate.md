## 摘要
我们展示了 TOI-789（TIC 300710077）的验证与动力学表征；这是一个围绕距离 43 pc 的 M3 型矮星的紧凑多行星系统。利用约 7 年的 TESS 测光数据（42 个扇区）、公开的地基后续观测（LCOGT、MEarth）和高分辨率成像（Gemini/Zorro、SOAR），我们使用 TRICERATOPS 统计验证了三颗类地行星：TOI-789 b（Rp = 1.18 Rearth，P = 5.45 d）、c（1.15 Rearth，8.04 d）和 d（1.40 Rearth，12.97 d）；在使用目标地基测光排除已分辨的邻近天体后，其假阳性概率和邻近假阳性概率均低于验证阈值。通过迭代凌星最小二乘搜索，我们发现第四颗此前未编目的行星候选体（P = 19.74 d，Rp ~ 0.88 Rearth），它是该系统最外侧且最温带的成员（S = 1.71 Searth，Teq ~ 292 K）；其较低的信噪比使其无法得到正式验证，因此我们将其作为待确认的候选体报告。动力学分析（SPOCK 与 N 体积分）表明，这是一个动力学冷、近共振的系统，仅在低偏心率（e < 0.05）下稳定，限制其稳定性的因素是偏心率而非质量；纳入第四颗候选体后，这一限制进一步收紧。这四个天体跨越金星带内的地球—金星转变，其中两颗已验证行星在平衡温度判据下处于温带区域，使该系统成为一个以已确认行星为锚点的潜在比较行星学实验室。较低的 TSM（约 3–5）和中等的主星亮度使大气表征难以实现。TOI-789 例证了数量上占主导的、围绕 M 矮星的紧凑类地行星系统群体：这类系统的表征受限于主星亮度，而动力学表征提供了一条互补途径。

## Abstract
We present the validation and dynamical characterization of TOI-789 (TIC 300710077), a compact multi-planet system around an M3 dwarf at 43 pc. Using ~7 yr of TESS photometry (42 sectors), public ground-based follow-up (LCOGT, MEarth), and high-resolution imaging (Gemini/Zorro, SOAR), we statistically validate three terrestrial planets with TRICERATOPS: TOI-789 b (Rp = 1.18 Rearth, P = 5.45 d), c (1.15 Rearth, 8.04 d), and d (1.40 Rearth, 12.97 d), with false-positive and nearby-false-positive probabilities below the validation thresholds once resolved neighbours are excluded using the on-target ground-based photometry. Through an iterative Transit Least Squares search we identify a fourth, previously uncatalogued planet candidate (P = 19.74 d, Rp ~ 0.88 Rearth), the outermost and most temperate member (S = 1.71 Searth, Teq ~ 292 K); its low signal-to-noise ratio precludes formal validation, so we report it as a candidate pending confirmation. A dynamical analysis (SPOCK and N-body integrations) indicates a dynamically cold, near-resonant system, stable only for low eccentricities (e < 0.05), eccentricity rather than mass limiting stability; including the fourth candidate tightens this further. The four objects span the Earth-Venus transition across the Venus zone, two validated planets falling in the temperate regime under the equilibrium-temperature criterion, making the system a potential comparative-planetology laboratory anchored on confirmed planets. The low TSM (~3-5) and moderate host brightness place atmospheric characterization beyond reach. TOI-789 exemplifies the numerically dominant population of compact terrestrial-planet systems around M dwarfs whose characterization is limited by host brightness, for which dynamical characterization offers a complementary route.
