## 摘要
当系统内多种实体相互协作、竞争或干扰时，就会出现非线性动力学。例如，心血管系统具有高度的复杂性。多导联心电信号由细胞协调的去极化和复极化过程产生，并表现出显著的非线性动力学。非线性动力系统难以通过传统的还原论方法来理解，即试图通过将分别分析的所有组成部分组合起来以理解系统行为。为应对系统复杂性，现代医疗系统正投资于先进的生理传感和患者监测，从而产生大数据。要充分实现大数据在医疗智能中的潜力，需要全新的方法来驾驭和利用复杂性。然而，现有的非线性动力学技术要么不关注医疗分析目标，要么无法有效分析大数据以提取有助于改善医疗服务的有用信息。迫切需要开发分析方法，充分挖掘生理系统中潜在的非线性动力学，以推动医疗服务发展，使其具备个性化、响应性和高质量等卓越特征。本章介绍了一些理论进展和工具，以推动非线性动力学原理在医疗保健中的应用。具体而言，我们聚焦于基于传感器的非线性动力学表征与建模（即多重分形分析和多尺度递归量化）。随后，探讨这些方法的当前发展与应用，以表征和利用心率变异性以及时空心电信号。

## Abstract
Nonlinear dynamics arise whenever multifarious entities of a system cooperate, compete, or interfere. For example, cardiovascular system involves a great level of complexity. Multi-lead ECG signals are generated through orchestrated depolarization and repolarization of cells and manifest significant nonlinear dynamics. Nonlinear dynamical systems defy understanding based on the traditional reductionist's approach, in which one attempts to understand a system's behavior by combining all constituent parts that have been analyzed separately. In order to cope with system complexity, modern healthcare systems are investing in advanced physiological sensing and patient monitoring, thereby giving rise to big data. Realizing the full potential of big data for healthcare intelligence requires fundamentally new methodologies to harness and exploit complexity. However, available nonlinear dynamics techniques are either not concerned with healthcare analytical objectives or fail to effectively analyze big data to extract useful information for improving healthcare services. There is an urgent need to develop analytical methodologies that fully exploit the underlying nonlinear dynamics in physiological systems for advancing healthcare services with exceptional features such as personalization, responsiveness, and superior quality. This chapter presents some theoretical developments and tools to advance the applications of nonlinear dynamics principles in health care. Specifically, we focus on sensor-based characterization and modeling of nonlinear dynamics (i.e., multifractal analysis and multiscale recurrence quantification). Then, current developments and applications of these methodologies are examined for characterizing and exploiting heart rate variability and space-time ECG signals.
