## 摘要
由加性噪声驱动的二次非线性系统在科学与工程中广泛出现，它们本身既是随机动力系统，也作为湍流的降阶模型（ROM）。我们提出随机物理约束算子推断（SPOpInf），这是一种非侵入式方法，可从数据中学习随机微分方程，即一个二次-线性漂移项和一个扩散幅值。与通过最小二乘拟合确定性漂移的算子推断（OpInf）不同，SPOpInf 最大化 Euler–Maruyama 增量似然，其中残差协方差本身是未知的，因此确定性和随机分量被联合校准；它通过在优化内部施加线性等式约束，精确地强制满足保能量的二次结构；并采用上三角参数化，从而消除完整二次张量的结构性不可辨识性。所得优化通过基于期望最大化（EM）的迭代学习算法求解，该算法交替更新漂移系数和噪声水平：每次迭代仅需对漂移进行廉价的加权最小二乘求解，并对噪声进行残差方差更新。在五个表现出间歇性和混沌的随机基准系统上，SPOpInf 比 OpInf 更准确地恢复确定性算子，以机器精度满足所施加的约束，准确估计噪声幅值，并再现长时间统计特性。将其应用于随机 Burgers 方程和准地转双涡旋流时，同一估计器可产生非侵入式随机 ROM，这些 ROM 在无约束替代方法会发散的长时间内保持稳定。

## Abstract
Quadratically nonlinear systems driven by additive noise arise throughout science and engineering, both as stochastic dynamical systems in their own right and as reduced-order models (ROMs) of turbulent flows. We propose stochastic physics-constrained operator inference (SPOpInf), a non-intrusive method that learns a stochastic differential equation, i.e., a quadratic--linear drift and a diffusion amplitude, from data. Unlike operator inference (OpInf), which fits a deterministic drift by least squares, SPOpInf maximizes an Euler--Maruyama increment likelihood in which the residual covariance is itself an unknown, so the deterministic and stochastic components are calibrated jointly; it enforces the energy-preserving quadratic structure exactly, through linear equality constraints imposed inside the optimization; and it works in an upper-triangular parametrization that removes the structural non-identifiability of the full quadratic tensor. The resulting optimization is solved by an expectation--maximization (EM)-based iterative learning algorithm that updates the drift coefficients and the noise levels in turn: each iteration requires only an inexpensive weighted least-squares solve for the drift and a residual-variance update for the noise. With five stochastic benchmark systems that show intermittency and chaos, SPOpInf recovers the deterministic operators more accurately than OpInf, satisfies the imposed constraints to machine precision, accurately estimates the noise amplitudes, and reproduces long-time statistics. Applied to a stochastic Burgers equation and a quasi-geostrophic double-gyre flow, the same estimator yields non-intrusive stochastic ROMs that remain stable over long times where unconstrained alternatives blow up.
