## 摘要
我们展示了对超短周期岩石行星 TOI-2431 b 的 HST/WFC3 G141 相位曲线观测，并在近红外波段探测到其次食。结合 TESS 的光学次食，这些测量约束了该行星的昼侧亮温、反射光贡献、反照率和热量再分配。我们在 TESS 的光学波段和 HST/WFC3 G141 的近红外波段均探测到次食，行星与恒星通量比分别为 37±10 ppm 和 69(+14/-13) ppm。假设反射光可忽略，联合 TESS+HST 拟合给出亮温 T_b,joint=2524(+77/-84) K，以及亮温比 R_joint=T_b,joint/T_p,max=0.980(+0.032/-0.034)，与零反照率、无再分配极限 T_p,max=2574(+36/-35) K 一致。HST 相位曲线与零夜侧辐射和无明显热点偏移一致，表明昼夜间热量分配效率低下。在这种热辐射主导的解释下，TOI-2431 b 处于零反照率、无再分配极限，且与此前观测到的熔岩世界形成对比。然而，在光学和近红外波段，热辐射与反射光存在简并。对于几何反照率 A_g=0.1，反射光会在每个波段约贡献 11 ppm，使温度降至 T_b,joint(A_g=0.1)=2407(+91/-102) K。需要更长波长的观测来分离热辐射成分。计划中的 JWST/MIRI LRS 观测有望显著降低这种简并，并为热量再分配以及可能的硅酸盐大气光谱特征提供更直接约束。这标志着首次使用 HST 探测到岩石系外行星的次食，并确立了 TOI-2431 b 作为研究熔岩世界性质的基准目标。

## Abstract
We present an HST/WFC3 G141 phase-curve observation of the ultra-short-period rocky planet TOI-2431 b and detect its secondary eclipse in the near infrared. Combined with the TESS optical eclipse, these measurements constrain the planet's dayside brightness temperature, reflected light contribution, albedo, and heat redistribution. We detect the eclipse in the optical and near infrared wavelengths of TESS and HST/WFC3 G141, with a planet-to-star flux ratio of 37+/-10 ppm and 69(+14/-13) ppm, respectively. Assuming negligible reflected light, the joint TESS+HST fit yields a brightness temperature T_b,joint=2524(+77/-84) K and a brightness temperature ratio of R_joint=T_b,joint/T_p,max=0.980(+0.032/-0.034) consistent with the zero-albedo, no-redistribution limit T_p,max=2574(+36/-35) K. The HST phase curve is consistent with zero nightside emission and no measurable hotspot offset, consistent with inefficient day-night heat distribution. Under this thermal-dominated interpretation, TOI-2431 b lies at the zero-albedo, no-redistribution limit and contrasts with previously observed lava worlds. At optical and near-infrared wavelengths, however, thermal emission is degenerate with reflected light. For a geometric albedo of A_g=0.1, reflected light would contribute approximately 11 ppm in each bandpass, reducing the temperature to T_b,joint(A_g=0.1)=2407(+91/-102) K. Longer-wavelength observations are required to isolate the thermal component. Planned JWST/MIRI LRS observations are expected to substantially reduce this degeneracy and provide more direct constraints on heat redistribution and possible silicate-atmosphere spectral features. This marks the first secondary eclipse detection of a rocky exoplanet using HST and establishes TOI-2431 b as a benchmark target for studying properties of lava worlds.

- **观测手段的作用**：次食（secondary eclipse）测量昼侧行星-恒星通量比；相位曲线约束昼夜亮度对比、夜侧辐射与最亮点相对星下点的经度偏移，从而探测热量再分配与大气动力学。

### 2.2 光变曲线拟合模型
- 总体模型：F(t) = F_transit(t) · F_emission(t) · F_sys,o(t) · F_sys,v(t)。
- 发射项由归一化到 1（食内）的 batman 次食模型加余弦/正弦相位项构成：F_pc(t) = (AmpCos/2)[cos(2π(t−t_sec)/P_orb) − 1] + (AmpSin/2) sin(2π(t−t_sec)/P_orb)。
- **参数化意义**：(AmpCos, AmpSin) = (1, 0) 即零夜侧通量、无相位偏移；振幅 Amp = √(AmpCos² + AmpSin²)，偏移 φ = atan2(AmpSin, AmpCos)，Amp = 1 表示峰谷振幅等于食深。
- 系统学：访问期长趋势测试线性/二次/指数/对数四种形式；轨道期 ramp 用指数函数，并允许首个拟合轨道有额外 ramp 项；正/反向扫描有方向依赖的常数偏移。
