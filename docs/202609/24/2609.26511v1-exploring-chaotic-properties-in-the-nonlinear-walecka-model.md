## 摘要
本文研究非线性 Walecka 模型（NLWM），亦称量子强子动力学（QHD）的动力学性质；该模型通过核子-介子相互作用为描述核物质提供了一个相对论框架。虽然原始线性模型成功刻画了核物质的定性特征，但它高估了核压缩性；因此，人们采用在标量场 (σ) 中引入三次和四次自相互作用项的非线性扩展，以更好地与实验数据相符。通过将自洽有效质量方程视为一种迭代映射，本研究探讨了周期轨道和混沌区域等复杂行为的出现。利用非线性动力学工具，包括返回映射和分岔图，我们刻画了经由倍周期分岔级联通向混沌的路径，并识别出参数空间混沌区域中存在的“shrimps”稳定等周期岛。此外，我们讨论了这些发现的物理意义，指出危机现象和结构收敛可能作为标量平均场中宏观重排的动力学特征，并可能影响中子星等极端环境中致密核物质的稳定性和状态方程（EOS）。

## Abstract
This paper investigates dynamical properties of the Nonlinear Walecka Model (NLWM), also known as Quantum Hadrodynamics (QHD), which provides a relativistic framework for describing nuclear matter through nucleon-meson interactions. While the original linear model successfully captures qualitative features of nuclear matter, it overestimates nuclear compressibility; consequently, nonlinear extensions incorporating cubic and quartic self-interaction terms in the scalar field $(σ)$ are employed to achieve better agreement with experimental data. By treating the self consistent effective-mass equation as an iterative mapping, the study explores the emergence of complex behaviors such as periodic orbits and chaotic regimes. Using tools from nonlinear dynamics, including return maps and bifurcation diagrams, we characterize routes to chaos via period doubling cascades and identify the presence of "shrimps" stable isoperiodic islands within chaotic regions of the parameter space. Furthermore, we discusses the physical implications of these findings, suggesting that crisis phenomena and structural convergence may serve as dynamical signatures of macroscopic rearrangements in the scalar mean field, potentially influencing the stability and equation of state (EOS) of dense nuclear matter in extreme environments like neutron stars.
