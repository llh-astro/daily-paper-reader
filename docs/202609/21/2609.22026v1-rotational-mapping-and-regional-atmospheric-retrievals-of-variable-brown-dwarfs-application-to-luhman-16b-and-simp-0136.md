## 摘要
我们提出了一个贝叶斯框架，用于将时间分辨光谱转换为由自转推断的区域光谱，并为可变褐矮星提供大气约束。我们推导了一个解析映射框架，该框架通过边缘化物理参数和模型复杂性，实现了基于证据优化的正则化与不确定性传播。我们提供了从推断的表面图中选择特征光谱端元的方法，并强调了分析这些由自转推断的区域光谱相对于盘面积分的时间分辨观测的优势，同时明确考虑自转映射固有的几何简并性。我们将该框架应用于已发表的 Luhman 16B 和 SIMP 0136 的 JWST/NIRSpec 自转监测数据。对于这两个天体，我们识别出主导光谱区域，并反演出云结构、热梯度和化学组成的区域变化，这些变化与云辐射反馈一致。此外，我们证明了临边昏暗在推导表面图中的重要性。这些结果建立了一种统计可控的方法，将自转光谱变化性与大气非均匀性联系起来，并为未来巨行星大气的自转映射和区域大气研究提供了可能。

## Abstract
We present a Bayesian framework for converting time-resolved spectroscopy into rotation-inferred regional spectra and atmospheric constraints for variable brown dwarfs. We derive an analytic mapping framework with evidence-optimized regularization and uncertainty propagation by marginalizing over physical parameters and model complexities. We provide methods for selecting characteristic spectral end-members from the inferred surface maps, and highlight the advantage of analyzing these rotation-inferred regional spectra over the disk-integrated time-resolved observations, while explicitly accounting for the geometric degeneracies inherent to rotational mapping. We apply this framework to published JWST/NIRSpec rotational monitoring of Luhman 16B and SIMP 0136. For both objects, we identify dominant spectral regions and retrieve regional variations in cloud structure, thermal gradients, and chemistry that are consistent with cloud radiative feedback. In addition, we demonstrate the importance of limb darkening in deriving surface maps. These results establish a statistically controlled method for linking rotational spectral variability to atmospheric heterogeneity and enable future rotational mapping and regional atmospheric studies of giant planet atmospheres.
