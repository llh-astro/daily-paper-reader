## 摘要
围绕大质量白矮星（$M_{\rm WD} > 0.8\,{\rm M}_\odot$）的残余行星系统本质上稀少，且仍缺乏充分刻画。作为中等质量 B 型和 A 型恒星的演化终点，这些白矮星为理解更高质量前身星周围行星系统的形成与存续提供了宝贵约束。在缺乏大型专用光谱巡天的情况下，极低分辨率的 Gaia XP 光谱为识别金属污染白矮星候选体提供了有力手段。我们对从 Gaia XP 光谱中选出的、大质量且金属污染的白矮星候选体进行了低分辨率光学光谱（R=500–4000）后续观测，主要搜寻 Ca II H & K 吸收，以扩充有效温度低于约 15,000 K 的大质量残余行星系统宿主样本。我们发现 Gaia XP 光谱能够高效识别金属污染白矮星，在我们样本的高置信度候选体（分类器概率 $P_{\rm DZ} > 0.65$）中，确认光球层金属的回收率为 82$\pm$5%（72 个目标中的 59 个）。然而，由 $G$、$G_{BP}$ 和 $G_{RP}$ 宽带测光导出的 Gaia 测光质量不足以识别质量最大的系统，因为可靠的质量测定需要详细的富金属大气拟合。我们展示了一组经确认既大质量又金属污染的冷白矮星，表明 Gaia XP 光谱能够显著扩大白矮星系外行星宿主样本。我们的搜寻促成了一个新凌星碎屑系统的发现，SDSS J1624+5404，这是迄今已发表的第 21 个此类系统，也是第 8 个显示周期性凌星的系统。

## Abstract
Remnant planetary systems around massive white dwarfs ($M_{\rm WD} > 0.8\,{\rm M}_\odot$) are intrinsically rare and remain poorly characterized. As the evolutionary endpoints of intermediate-mass B- and A-type stars, these white dwarfs provide valuable constraints on the formation and survival of planetary systems around higher-mass progenitors. In the absence of large, dedicated spectroscopic surveys, extremely-low-resolution Gaia XP spectra provide a powerful means of identifying candidate metal-polluted white dwarfs. We obtained follow-up observations with low-resolution optical spectroscopy (R=500-4000) to search primarily for Ca II H & K absorption in massive, metal-polluted white dwarf candidates selected from Gaia XP spectra to increase the sample of massive remnant planetary system hosts among objects with effective temperatures below $\sim15{,}000$ K. We find that Gaia XP spectra recover metal-polluted white dwarfs efficiently, confirming 82$\pm$5% recovery of photospheric metals (59 of 72 targets) in high-confidence candidates of our sample (with classifier probability $P_{\rm DZ} > 0.65$). However, Gaia photometric masses derived from $G$, $G_{BP}$, and $G_{RP}$ broad-band photometry are not sufficient to identify the most massive systems, as reliable mass determinations require detailed metal-enriched atmospheric fits. We present a selection of cool white dwarfs confirmed to be both massive and metal polluted, demonstrating that Gaia XP spectra can substantially expand the sample of white dwarf exoplanet hosts. Our search led to the discovery of a new transiting debris system, SDSS J1624+5404, the 21st published system to date and eighth to show periodic transits.
