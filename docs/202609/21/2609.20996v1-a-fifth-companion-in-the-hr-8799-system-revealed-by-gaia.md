## 摘要
HR 8799是标杆性的年轻多行星系统，拥有四颗通过直接成像发现的巨行星，它们运行于温暖的内带与寒冷的外碎片环之间。然而，几个天文单位（au）的区域在观测上一直难以触及，超出了传统高对比度成像的能力范围，并且由于恒星脉动而给径向速度技术带来挑战。在此，我们将Gaia绝对天体测量以及Hipparcos-Gaia自行异常，与径向速度和高对比度成像相结合，揭示了一颗额外的近距亚恒星伴星。容许解覆盖约0.3–6 au，后验概率峰值约在2–3 au，质量约为10–14个木星质量。动力学建模表明，这样一颗低偏心率的伴星可以与四颗外层行星长期存在的共振链共存，并塑造温暖碎片带的内边缘，从而将行星架构与碎片盘形态联系起来。我们的结果弥合了HR 8799中一个长期存在的探测空白，并展示了Gaia天体测量在揭示中等间距巨行星方面的能力，这些行星可能仅靠径向速度或直接成像仍无法探测到。

## Abstract
HR 8799 is the benchmark young multi-planet system, hosting four directly imaged giant planets orbiting between a warm inner belt and a cold outer debris ring. However, the few-au region has remained observationally inaccessible, lying beyond the reach of conventional high-contrast imaging and challenging for radial-velocity techniques due to stellar pulsations. Here we combine Gaia absolute astrometry and Hipparcos-Gaia proper-motion anomalies with radial velocities and high-contrast imaging to reveal an additional close-in substellar companion. The allowed solutions span approx. 0.3-6 au, with posteriors peaking at approx. 2-3 au and with masses around 10-14 Jupiter masses. Dynamical modelling shows that such a companion with a low eccentricity can coexist with the long-lived resonant chain of the four outer planets, and sculpt the inner edge of the warm debris belt, linking the planet architecture to the debris-disk morphology. Our results close a long-standing detection gap in HR 8799 and demonstrate the power of Gaia astrometry to uncover massive planets at intermediate separations that may remain undetectable with radial velocities or direct imaging alone.
