## 摘要
Kepler-51 系统拥有凌星超级蓬松行星，其极低的体密度对结构模型构成挑战，这可能是因为未分辨的环使其推断半径膨胀。我们将星体密度剖面与顾及 TTV 的开普勒长节奏测光凌星拟合相结合，以检验这一假设。对于 Kepler-51b，我们测得密度比 Ψ≡ρ_{⋆,obs}/ρ_{⋆,true}=1.18±0.06，偏离 Ψ=1 达 2.9σ；对于 Kepler-51d，我们得到 Ψ=1.04±0.08（0.6σ），与无异常情况一致。TTV 导出的动力学约束不支持行星 b 存在未建模偏心率（p=0.007），但行星 d 仍可行（p=0.33）。Kepler-51b 的偏移也与未建模环的 PhotoRing（PR）效应相容。利用几何环凌星正向模型和贝叶斯反演，我们识别出能够重现凌星观测量和测得密度比的环构型。偏好解与紧凑、中等倾角的环一致，这些环使真实体密度提高 2–5 倍。相反，未出现大的 PR 偏移，仅凭档案测光数据就以 99% 可信度排除 b 的延展、光学厚、高倾角环，并以 70% 可信度排除 d 的这类环。因此，这些天体可能是“蓬松环行星”，尽管最近对 Kepler-51d 的 JWST 观测未显示不透明环的直接特征。虽然所需的碰撞参数和恒星密度与独立约束存在张力——这可归因于开普勒 30 分钟采样节奏——本研究首次完整应用了 PR 效应，作为方法学演示和排除实验，而非环存在的证据。所有代码均已公开，可支持对其他系统的应用。

## Abstract
The Kepler-51 system hosts transiting super-puff planets whose extremely low bulk densities challenge structural models, possibly because unresolved rings inflate their inferred radii. We test this hypothesis by combining asterodensity profiling with TTV-aware transit fits of Kepler long-cadence photometry. For Kepler-51b we measure a density ratio $Ψ\equivρ_{\star,\mathrm{obs}}/ρ_{\star,\mathrm{true}} = 1.18\pm0.06$, a $2.9σ$ offset from $Ψ=1$; for Kepler-51d we find $Ψ=1.04\pm0.08$ ($0.6σ$), consistent with no anomaly. Unmodelled eccentricity is disfavoured for planet b by TTV-derived dynamical constraints ($p=0.007$) but remains viable for planet d ($p=0.33$). The offset of Kepler-51b is also compatible with the PhotoRing (PR) effect of unmodeled rings. Using a geometric ringed-transit forward model and Bayesian retrieval, we identify ring configurations that reproduce the transit observables and the measured density ratios. The preferred solutions are consistent with compact, moderately inclined rings that raise the true bulk densities by factors of $2-5$. Conversely, the absence of a large PR offset excludes extended, optically thick, high-obliquity rings at $99\%$ credibility for b and $70\%$ for d from archival photometry alone. These objects may thus be "puff ringed planets" although recent JWST observations of Kepler-51d show no direct signature of an opaque ring. While the required impact parameters and stellar densities show tension with independent constraints -attributable to Kepler's 30-minute cadence- this study presents the first complete application of the PR effect, offered as a methodological demonstration and exclusion experiment rather than as evidence that rings are present. All code is publicly available to support applications to other systems.
