## 摘要
最近发射的南希·格雷斯·罗曼空间望远镜（Roman）很快将开始观测，这些观测有望彻底改变我们对系外行星族群统计的理解。Roman 的银河核球时域巡天（GBTDS）将通过凌星和微引力透镜技术观测银河核球，为在致密且运动学炽热的恒星环境中研究热木星以及长周期/自由漂浮系外行星提供了独特机会。在这篇快报中，我们量化了反复的脉冲式恒星飞掠如何截断核球中行星系统的外缘。我们发现，对于低质量宿主周围的宽轨道（≳250 au），飞掠会导致在约 Gyr 时标上的抛射。倘若这些宽轨道上确实有行星，这些抛射很可能贡献于 Roman 所探测的自由漂浮行星族群。在约 10 au 之外幸存的行星会被置于偏心且轨道面错位的轨道上。我们表明，尽管系统经历许多近距离交会，其演化由单次最强扰动主导；行星轨道（即使是那些免于抛射的轨道）也会经历超扩散（即 Lévy 飞行）。我们讨论了结果对适于 Roman 凌星搜索的热木星起源的意义。我们的工作提供了一个定量基线，未来动力学研究可在此基础上更好地理解银河核球中行星系统的演化。

## Abstract
The recently launched Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope (\textit{Roman}) will soon begin observations that promise to revolutionize our understanding of exoplanet demographics. \textit{Roman}'s Galactic Bulge Time-Domain Survey (GBTDS) will observe the Galactic Bulge via transit and microlensing techniques, providing a unique opportunity to study hot Jupiters as well as long-period/free-floating exoplanets in a dense and kinematically hot stellar environment. In this Letter, we quantify how repeated, impulsive stellar flybys truncate the outskirts of planetary systems in the Bulge. We find that flybys result in ejections on $\sim$Gyr timescales for wide orbits (${\gtrsim} 250\ \text{au}$) around low-mass hosts. Provided these wide orbits are populated, these ejections likely contribute to the population of free-floating planets probed by \textit{Roman}. Surviving planets outside of ${\sim} 10$ au are placed on eccentric and misaligned orbits. We show that, though systems suffer many close encounters, the evolution is dominated by the single strongest perturbation; planetary orbits (even those that evade ejection) undergo superdiffusion (i.e. a Lévy flight). We comment on the implications of our results for the origin of hot Jupiters amenable to $\textit{Roman}$'s transit search. Our work serves as a quantitative baseline from which future dynamical studies can build to better understand the evolution of planetary systems in the Galactic Bulge.
