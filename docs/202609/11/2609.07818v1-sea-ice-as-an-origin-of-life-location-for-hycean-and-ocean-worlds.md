## 摘要
可能拥有深海洋的水世界处于当前可观测系外行星宜居性研究的前沿。宜居性的标准要求是存在能量源、营养源和溶剂，所有这些都可以在拥有深层液态水海洋的系外行星上得到满足。这类行星可能数量众多，从氢海世界和海洋世界到水含量较高的类地大小行星不等。然而，生命起源还需要额外条件，即一种将营养物质积累到前生命化学所需高浓度的机制，以及驱动该化学过程的能量源。传统上认为，深海，以及在某些情况下其下方的高压冰层，会阻止达到所需的浓度。在此，我们提出一个可能使营养物质浓缩到足以让生命起源成为可能的地点——开放海洋与表面冰架之间的界面。利用三维大气环流模型，我们研究了若干可能拥有昼侧海冰的候选宜居系外行星对应的情况。我们讨论行星气候如何影响海冰的性质和行为。我们研究小尺度成冰和融化过程将营养物质浓缩到前生命可利用水平的能力，并探索陨石撞击体碎片为前生命化学提供平台的可能性。我们发现，这两种机制都可能合理地提供浓缩的化学原料，供前生命化学使用；这为评估具有深海洋行星的宜居性前景增添了重要的新视角。

## Abstract
Water worlds with potential deep oceans are at the forefront of currently observable exoplanetary habitability. The standard requirements for habitability are the presence of an energy source, a source of nutrients, and a solvent, all of which can be met in exoplanets with deep liquid water oceans. Such planets may be numerous, ranging from hycean worlds and ocean worlds to terrestrial-size planets with enhanced water content. However, abiogenesis requires additional conditions, namely a mechanism to accumulate nutrients to the high concentrations required for prebiotic chemistry, and an energy source to drive the chemistry. A deep ocean, and in some cases the high-pressure ice layers underneath, has traditionally been thought to prevent the required concentrations. Here we outline a location where nutrients could be concentrated in sufficiently high quantities to make abiogenesis possible -- at the interface between the open ocean and the surface ice shelf. Using a 3D General Circulation Model, we explore cases corresponding to several candidate habitable exoplanets which might have dayside sea ice. We discuss how the planet's climate influences the nature and behaviour of the sea ice. We investigate the ability of small-scale ice formation and melting processes to concentrate nutrients to prebiotically useful levels, and also explore the potential for meteoritic impactor fragments to provide a platform for prebiotic chemistry. We find that both of these mechanisms may plausibly provide concentrated chemical feedstock for prebiotic chemistry, which adds an important new perspective when evaluating the prospects of habitability in planets with deep oceans.
