## 摘要
在太阳附近观测到的恒星未必诞生于该处。径向混合会在银河系盘上重新分布恒星族群，使其当前位置与形成环境脱钩。我们概述了一个框架，该框架将银河系化学演化模型与广义加性模型相结合，以推断恒星诞生半径。我们将其应用于1327颗以薄盘为主的宿主星，这些宿主星具有均一化的年龄、Gaia DR3天体测量数据和SWEET-Cat大气参数。我们发现，巨行星系统优先示踪富金属的内盘诞生环境，而仅含岩质行星的系统则中心集中程度较低；并且径向位移与探测到的行星多重性之间没有明确关系。向银河系内侧迁移的行星宿主星，其系统具有更大的轨道间距；然而，这可能部分反映了已知的行星轨道性质对宿主星金属丰度的依赖，而非银河系恒星迁移的因果效应。这些结果说明，银河系考古学可以通过将行星系统与其可能的诞生环境联系起来，来补充系外行星统计特征。

## Abstract
Stars observed in the solar vicinity were not necessarily born there. Radial mixing can redistribute stellar populations across the Galactic disc, decoupling their present-day locations from the environments in which they formed. We summarise a framework that combines Galactic chemical evolution models with a generalised additive model to infer stellar birth radii. We apply it to 1327 predominantly thin-disc planet hosts with homogeneous ages, \textit{Gaia} DR3 astrometry, and \texttt{SWEET-Cat} atmospheric parameters. We find that giant-planet systems preferentially trace metal-rich inner-disc birth environments, whereas rocky-only systems are less centrally concentrated; and no clear relation between radial displacement and detected planet multiplicity. Planet-host stars that migrate towards the inner parts of the Galaxy have systems with larger orbital separations; however, this may partly reflect the known dependence of planetary orbital properties on host-star metallicity rather than a causal effect of Galactic stellar migration. These results illustrate how Galactic archaeology can complement exoplanet demographics by linking planetary systems to their probable birth environments.
