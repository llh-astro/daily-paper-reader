## 摘要
对行星形成中的星周盘的天文观测表明，星子由尺寸为0.1 mm至1 cm的尘埃聚集体形成，通常称为“卵石”。当此类卵石在水雪线之外吸积时，由此形成的含冰星子会发生水–岩分异，并形成多孔的卵石堆核，其空隙被液态水饱和。这种水的循环增强核部热输运，抑制由放射性核素衰变引起的温度升高。在本研究中，我们通过模拟含冰星子的热演化，并将结果与从小行星龙宫返回样品中识别出的水溶液形成的矿物的沉淀年龄和温度进行比较，来约束龙宫母星子的吸积年龄和组成卵石尺寸。我们的数值结果表明，龙宫母星子在富钙铝包体形成后2.0 Myr内完成吸积。推断出的早期吸积年龄支持如下假说：龙宫母星子形成早于大多数含球粒的碳质球粒陨石母星子，这可能解释龙宫样品中缺乏球粒的现象。我们还发现，母星子的核部由不大于几毫米的卵石组成。如此小的卵石尺寸与在CO$_{2}$雪线之外形成的星子的理论预测一致。

## Abstract
Astronomical observations of planet-forming circumstellar disks indicate that planetesimals form from 0.1-mm- to 1-cm-sized dust aggregates, commonly called ``pebbles.'' When such pebbles accrete beyond the water snow line, the resulting icy planetesimals undergo water--rock differentiation and develop porous pebble-pile cores whose voids are saturated with liquid water. Circulation of this water enhances heat transport in the core, suppressing the temperature rise caused by the decay of radionuclides. In this study, we constrain the accretion age and constituent pebble size of Ryugu's parent planetesimal by modeling the thermal evolution of icy planetesimals and comparing the results with the precipitation ages and temperatures of aqueously formed minerals identified in samples returned from asteroid Ryugu. Our numerical results suggest that Ryugu's parent planetesimal accreted within 2.0 Myr of the formation of calcium--aluminum-rich inclusions. The inferred early accretion age supports the hypothesis that Ryugu's parent planetesimal formed earlier than most chondrule-bearing carbonaceous chondrite parent planetesimals, potentially explaining the absence of chondrules in Ryugu samples. We also found that the core of the parent planetesimal was composed of pebbles no larger than a few millimeters. Such small pebble sizes are consistent with theoretical predictions for planetesimals formed beyond the CO$_{2}$ snow line.
