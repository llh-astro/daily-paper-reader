## 摘要
超短周期（USP）岩质系外行星的昼侧温度可高到足以维持永久性岩浆洋，处于内部地球物理与大气化学的交汇点。熔融表面与脱气大气之间的耦合反馈可以维持或增强挥发分包层，而恒星相互作用则可侵蚀它。要理解哪种结果占主导及其可观测印记，需要针对处于不同热演化阶段的行星采取多目标方法。我们给出了 JWST 第4周期 8864 项目五个目标的预测：TOI-1807 b、TOI-2260 b、TOI-431 b、TOI-6255 b 和 TOI-2431 b。利用 PROTEUS 耦合内部-大气框架，我们构建了一个模拟网格，并将结果划分为六类，分类依据是最终内部熔融状态以及行星是否保留足够厚、可探测、能将热量再分配至夜侧的大气。对于保留不可忽略挥发分包层的目标，我们的模型预测，当表面熔融时，大多数物种的分压更高，但 S₂ 除外；其在固态情形下的增强表明它或可作为内部熔融状态的示踪物。尽管一些目标的结果分布在多种情景中，但大多数倾向于裸岩端元，全球熔融分数 ≤ 20%，且大气保留对逃逸效率敏感。我们的分析揭示了一个最低逃逸效率阈值：低于该阈值时，挥发分包层可在能量限制逃逸下存活，从而约束了受辐照岩质行星上大气存活所需的条件。这些预测将指导 MIRI-LRS 相位曲线观测的解释，并确定哪些目标与特征最能区分相互竞争的地球物理状态。

## Abstract
Ultra-short-period (USP) rocky exoplanets can have dayside temperatures high enough to maintain permanent magma oceans, sitting at the intersection of interior geophysics and atmospheric chemistry. Coupled feedbacks between the molten surface and outgassed atmosphere can sustain or enhance a volatile envelope, while stellar interactions can erode it. Understanding which outcome prevails, and its observable imprint, requires a multi-target approach across planets at different stages of thermal evolution. We present predictions for the five targets of JWST Cycle 4 program 8864: TOI-1807 b, TOI-2260 b, TOI-431 b, TOI-6255 b, and TOI-2431 b. Using the PROTEUS coupled interior-atmosphere framework, we construct a simulation grid and classify outcomes into six categories, defined by the final interior melt state and by whether the planet retains a detectable atmosphere thick enough to redistribute heat to the nightside. For targets retaining a non-negligible volatile envelope, our models predict higher partial pressures for most species when the surface is molten, except for S$_{2}$, whose enhancement in the solid regime suggests it may serve as a tracer of interior melt state. Despite some targets showing outcomes across multiple scenarios, most tend toward a bare-rock end-member, with global melt fraction $\leq$ 20\% and atmospheric retention sensitive to escape efficiency. Our analysis reveals a minimum escape efficiency threshold below which volatile envelopes survive under energy-limited escape, constraining the conditions required for atmosphere survival on irradiated rocky planets. These predictions will guide interpretation of MIRI-LRS phase curve observations and identify which targets and features best discriminate between competing geophysical states.
