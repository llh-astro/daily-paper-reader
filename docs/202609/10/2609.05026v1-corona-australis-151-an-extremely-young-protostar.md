## 摘要
背景。前恒星核是恒星和行星系统的诞生地，但它们寿命短暂，因为致密核演化的初始阶段、坍缩以及原恒星种子的形成都很快。为建立太阳邻域中真正前恒星核的星表，我们利用 APEX 观测，在 Herschel 观测到的致密核中识别出动力学演化的核。其中之一，Corona Australis 151，因其中心峰结构而引人注目：在中心 500–1000 au 内密度超过 10^7 cm^-3，且氘化程度高（N2D+/N2H+≈0.5），暗示其处于演化的前恒星阶段。目标。Corona Australis 151 看似是一个演化的前恒星核，但在某些探测到的谱线中存在宽翼，以及 Herschel 的暂定 70 μm 探测，部分挑战了其前恒星阶段。我们旨在评估其演化阶段。方法。我们分析了新的 ALMA 连续谱发射和若干谱线示踪剂（包括典型外流示踪剂）数据，分辨率为约 150–200 au，示踪包层。结果。我们揭示了一个紧凑而年轻的外流（投影动力学年龄约 500 yr），由 SiO、CO 和 H2CO 发射示踪。连续谱发射示踪出一个尺度约 1000 au、质量为 0.33 M_sun 的包层结构（假设 Tdust=20 K）。结论。Corona Australis 151 是一颗极其年轻的原恒星，可能是太阳邻域中已知最年轻的原恒星之一，因此是研究恒星形成黎明时期化学和动力学演化的新实验室。

## Abstract
Context. Prestellar cores are the birthplace of stars and planetary systems, but they are short-lived objects, since the initial stages of dense core evolution, collapse, and the formation of a protostellar seed are fast. In an effort to build a catalogue of bona-fide prestellar cores in the Solar neighbourhood, we used APEX observations to identify dynamically evolved cores among dense cores observed with Herschel. One of them, Corona Australis 151, stood out because of its centrally peaked structure, with densities above 1e7cm-3 in the central 500-1000 au, and high deuteration levels (N2D+/N2H+~0.5), suggestive of an evolved, prestellar stage.   Aims. Corona Australis 151 appears to be an evolved prestellar core, but the presence of broad wings in some of the detected lines and a tentative 70micron detection with Herschel partially challenges its prestellar stage. We intend to assess its evolutionary stage.   Methods. We analyse new ALMA data of the continuum emission and of several line tracers (including typical outflow tracers) at a resolution of~150-200au, tracing the envelope.   Results. We unveil the presence of a compact and young outflow (projected dynamical age:~500yr), traced by SiO, CO, and H2CO emission. The continuum emission traces an envelope structure of size~1000 au and mass 0.33M_sun(assuming Tdust=20 K).   Conclusions. Corona Australis 151 is an extremely young protostar, possibly one of the youngest known in the Solar neighbourhood, and hence a new laboratory to study the chemical and dynamical evolution at the dawn of star formation.
