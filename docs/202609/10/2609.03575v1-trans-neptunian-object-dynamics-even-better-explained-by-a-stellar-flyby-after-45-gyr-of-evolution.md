## 摘要
海王星外天体（TNO）与行星一起由扁平的气体和尘埃盘形成，但现在大多以倾斜、偏心的轨道绕太阳运行。对TNO轨道的一种解释是另一颗恒星的近距离飞掠。一颗质量 $M_p =$ 0.8 Msun、$q_p =$ 110 au、$i_p =$70° 的恒星飞掠与观测相符。然而，当太阳还年轻并仍处于其诞生星团中时，这类近距离交会更为频繁。假设该飞掠发生在当时，我们使用数值 $N$ 体模拟来模拟自太阳形成以来的4.56 Gyr（45.6亿年）中，TNO轨道因与行星相互作用而发生的变化。我们发现，冷柯伊伯带区域损失了其初始族群的约80%，热柯伊伯带族群损失了约40%。约7%–8%的TNO被注入行星区域，但其中几乎所有（99%）随后都被抛射出去。相比之下，遥远类赛德娜天体（$q >$ 60 au）的轨道参数基本保持不变。令人惊讶的是，总的来说，这些变化甚至进一步改善了对观测到的TNO族群的拟合，从而加强了对太阳系曾发生一次近距离飞掠的论证。剩余的差异涉及冷族群的位置以及赛德娜族群的倾角分布。我们讨论了可能的原因以及解决这些问题的步骤。

## Abstract
The Trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs) formed together with the planets from a flat disc of gas and dust but now orbit the Sun mostly on inclined, eccentric orbits. One explanation for the TNOs' orbits is a close flyby of another star. One with a mass of $M_p =$0.8 Msun at $q_p =$ 110 au and $i_p =$70° fits the observations. However, such close encounters were more frequent when the Sun was young and still part of its birth cluster. Assuming that the flyby happened then, we use numerical $N$-body simulations to model how the TNOs' orbits changed due to interactions with the planets over the 4.56 Gyr since the Sun formed. We find that the cold Kuiper belt region lost about 80% and the hot Kuiper belt population about 40% of its initial population. About 7% --8% of the TNOs were injected into the planet region, but almost all (99%) were ejected afterwards. By contrast, the orbital parameters of distant Sedna-like objects ($q >$60 au) remained basically unchanged. Surprisingly, in sum, these changes improve the fit to the observed TNO population even more, strengthening the argument for a close flyby to the Solar System. The remaining differences concern the location of the cold population and the inclination distribution of the Sedna population. We discuss possible reasons and steps to resolve these issues.
