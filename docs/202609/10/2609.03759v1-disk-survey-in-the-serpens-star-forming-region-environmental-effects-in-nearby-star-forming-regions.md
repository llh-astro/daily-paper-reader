## 摘要
原行星盘所处的外部环境调控着盘的演化。外部辐照会加热并蒸发气体，而频繁的恒星交会会截断盘，二者均可减小盘的尺寸和质量。对具有不同环境的邻近恒星形成区开展盘巡天，有助于我们理解外部环境如何影响盘演化。Serpens 恒星形成区被认为致密但未受到大质量恒星的强辐照，是研究动力学效应对盘性质影响的理想实验室。我们旨在通过比较 Serpens 与其他邻近恒星形成区的盘质量和三维恒星密度，研究动力学相互作用如何改变盘尘埃质量。我们使用 ALMA 以 0.25 角秒分辨率对 Serpens 中 321 个从 I 类到 III 类的年轻恒星体进行巡天。我们在光学薄假设下从毫米波观测测量盘尘埃质量，并利用 Gaia 数据重新计算三维恒星密度。我们将相同方法应用于其他邻近恒星形成区，以便与 Serpens 直接比较。Serpens 的累积盘尘埃质量分布与年龄相近的邻近恒星形成区（如 Lupus 和 Taurus）相似。结合对三维恒星密度的重新评估，这表明 Serpens 不太可能经历过能够产生较低盘尘埃质量的强潮汐截断。我们还发现，当仅考虑被明确认定为年轻（1-2 Myr）子星团成员的盘时，Ophiuchus 和 Corona Australis 中低盘尘埃质量的矛盾可以得到解决。没有 Gaia 证认的盘，尤其是在 Ophiuchus 中，在二维投影天平面上呈空间成团分布，并且比附近有 Gaia 证认的盘质量更低，这可能反映了在更早期演化阶段、更致密环境中的潮汐截断效应。

## Abstract
The external environment where protoplanetary disks are embedded regulates disk evolution. External irradiation, which heats and evaporates gas, as well as frequent stellar encounters, which truncate disks, can reduce disk sizes and masses. Disk surveys in nearby star-forming regions with various environments can help us understand how external environments impact disk evolution. The Serpens star-forming region, which is thought to be dense but not highly irradiated by massive stars, is an ideal laboratory to study the dynamical effects on disk properties.   We aim to study how dynamical interactions modify disk dust masses by comparing Serpens with other nearby star-forming regions through their disk masses and 3D stellar densities. We survey 321 young stellar objects from Class I to III in Serpens using ALMA at 0.25 arcsec. We measure disk dust masses from millimeter observations under the optically thin assumption and recompute the 3D stellar density using Gaia data. We apply the same method to other nearby star-forming regions for direct comparison with Serpens.   The cumulative disk dust mass distribution of Serpens is similar to those of similarly aged nearby star-forming regions, such as Lupus and Taurus. Along with re-assessment of the 3D stellar density, it suggests that Serpens is not likely to have experienced strong tidal truncation capable of producing lower disk dust masses. We also find that the low disk dust mass tension in Ophiuchus and Corona Australis can be resolved when only disks that have been explicitly identified as members of young (1-2 Myr) sub-clusters are considered. Disks without Gaia identifications, especially in Ophiuchus, are spatially clustered in the 2D projected sky plane and less massive than nearby disks with Gaia identifications, possibly tracing the effects of tidal truncation in denser environments at earlier evolutionary stages.
