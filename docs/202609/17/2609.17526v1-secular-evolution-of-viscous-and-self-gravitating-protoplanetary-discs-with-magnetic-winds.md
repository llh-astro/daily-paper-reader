## 摘要
传统上，人们认为盘在湍流黏性作用下演化，尽管该机制的重要性最近受到质疑。或者，也可以考虑磁盘风或引力不稳定性，但在给定演化阶段，这三种机制中哪一种贡献最大仍是一个未解问题。我们展示了在薄盘极限下、在盘寿命时间尺度上的原行星盘演化全局数值流体动力学模拟，其中包含质量与角动量输运的所有三种机制。我们计算引力、黏性和磁力矩以评估每种机制的贡献。我们发现，这些输运机制根据演化阶段在盘中占据不同的影响区域：早期阶段盘自引力的主导地位被磁盘风和黏性的盛行所取代，其中后者在盘的外围区域尤为重要。我们表明，盘质量的显著下降仅在嵌入阶段结束后才会发生。只有最强的风才能抑制盘的黏性扩展；否则，盘在模拟结束前会持续增大。螺旋结构有助于尘埃在盘中的暂时滞留。然而，这不足以在长时间尺度上防止尘埃耗竭。结果强调了采用综合方法对原行星盘进行长期模拟的重要性，不能将任何一种质量与角动量输运机制视为唯一机制。

## Abstract
Traditionally, the disc is believed to evolve under the influence of turbulent viscosity, although the importance of this mechanism has recently been questioned. Alternatively, magnetic disc wind or gravitational instability could be considered, but which of these three mechanisms contributes the most at a given evolutionary stage remains an open question. We present global numerical hydrodynamic simulations of protoplanetary disc evolution in the thin-disc limit on disc lifetime timescales, including all three mechanisms of mass and angular momentum transport. We calculate the gravitational, viscous, and magnetic torques to assess the contribution of each mechanism. We found that these transport mechanisms occupy distinct zones of influence in the disc depending on the stage of evolution: the dominance of disc self-gravity during the early phase is replaced by the prevalence of magnetic disc wind and viscosity, with the latter being especially relevant at the disc periphery. We show that a noticeable decrease in disc mass occurs only after the termination of the embedded phase. The suppression of disc viscous spreading is achieved only for the most intense wind; otherwise, the disc continues to grow in size till the end of simulations. The spiral structure contributes to the temporary retention of dust in the disc. However, this is insufficient to prevent the depletion of dust on long timescales. The results emphasize the importance of a comprehensive approach to long-term simulations of protoplanetary discs with no single mechanism of mass and angular momentum transport regarded as exclusive.
