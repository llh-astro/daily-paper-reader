## 摘要
K2-3系统是最独特的行星系统之一，因为它包含三颗凌星亚海王星，其半径依次递减，并横跨半径谷。此外，两颗外侧行星具有温带性质，这使该系统成为研究小型潜在宜居世界的性质和形成的绝佳实验室。我们展示了在ESPRESSO-GTO计划期间获得的新径向速度数据，并将其加入档案中的HARPS、HARPS-N和HIRES数据，总时间跨度达8年。这一合并数据集显著改善了对K2-3行星质量的测定。我们使用逐线算法分析光谱数据。我们将该时间序列与多维高斯过程框架相结合，以将40.5天的恒星自转与开普勒信号分离开来。我们使用两种活动指标测试了模型，以确保反演得到的行星半振幅不会因活动指标的选择而产生偏差。无论采用哪种活动指标，多维高斯过程框架都给出以下质量：Mb=6.61±0.47Me；Mc=2.23±0.68Me；Md=2.12±1.27Me。与先前工作相比，我们对K2-3b的探测显著性提高到原来的两倍，K2-3c的质量略有降低，而我们现在对K2-3d仅有边缘探测。行星在质量-半径图中的位置表明，K2-3b和c可能富含H/He或富含挥发物，而K2-3d的性质尚不明确。正向大气模型表明，单次JWST观测即可区分富含H/He的大气与水世界情景。该数据集例证了ESPRESSO探测亚米每秒径向速度信号以发现温带系外行星的能力。

## Abstract
The K2-3 system is one of the most unique planetary systems as it features three transiting sub-Neptunes in decreasing order of radii, spanning the radius valley. In addition to that, the two outer planets are temperate in nature making this system a perfect laboratory for investigating the nature and formation of small potentially habitable worlds. We present new radial velocity data obtained during the ESPRESSO-GTO programme to add to the archival HARPS, HARPS-N and HIRES data for a total time span of 8 years. This combined dataset significantly improves the masses of the K2-3 planets. We analyse the spectroscopic data with the line-by-line algorithm. We coupled this timeseries with a multi-dimensional Gaussian process framework to disentangle the 40.5-days stellar rotation from the Keplerian signals. We tested models using two activity indicators to ensure the retrieved semi-amplitudes of the planets are not biased by the choice of activity indicator. The multi-dimensional Gaussian process framework, with either activity indicator, yields the following masses: Mb=6.61+/-0.47Me; Mc=2.23+/-0.68Me; Md=2.12+/-1.27Me. Compared to previous works, our detection significance of K2-3b has increased by a factor of two, the mass of K2-3c has slightly decreased and we now have a marginal detection for K2-3d. The positions of the planets in the mass-radius diagram suggest either a H/He-rich or volatile-rich composition for both K2-3b and c, while the nature of K2-3d is ambiguous. A forward atmospheric model suggests that a single visit with JWST can distinguish between a H/He-rich atmosphere from a water-world scenario. This dataset exemplifies the capability of ESPRESSO to detect sub-metre-per-second radial velocity signals for the detection of temperate exoplanets.
