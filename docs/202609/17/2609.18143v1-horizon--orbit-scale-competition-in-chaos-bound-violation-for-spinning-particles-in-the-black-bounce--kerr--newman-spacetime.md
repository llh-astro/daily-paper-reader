## 摘要
本文通过在黑洞反弹--Kerr--Newman 时空中不稳定圆轨道上带电自旋试验粒子的局域径向不稳定性来研究混沌界限。我们的结果表明，界限的违反由局域轨道不稳定性尺度与表面引力尺度之间的竞争所支配，而黑洞背景和粒子动力学的变化为达到同一阈值穿越提供了两条互补途径。背景变化会影响这两个尺度，并且当表面引力比轨道不稳定性受到更强抑制时，可将系统驱动到违反界限的区域。相反，在固定背景下，表面引力保持不变，跨越阈值的转变由粒子参数引起的不稳定轨道动力学变化所驱动。因此，背景控制和探针动力学控制的途径代表统一的视界--轨道尺度竞争的两种实现，而非彼此独立的机制。在所考虑的粒子参数中，总角动量在控制局域径向不稳定性方面起主导作用，而自旋和电荷进一步改变不稳定性强度并移动混沌界限违反的阈值。

## Abstract
In this paper, we investigate the chaos bound through the local radial instability of charged spinning test particles on unstable circular orbits in the black-bounce--Kerr--Newman spacetime. Our results show that violation of the bound is governed by the competition between the local orbital-instability scale and the surface gravity scale, with variations of the black hole background and the particle dynamics providing two complementary routes to the same threshold crossing. Background variations affect both scales and can drive the system into the bound-violating regime when the surface gravity is suppressed more strongly than the orbital instability. At fixed background, by contrast, the surface gravity remains unchanged, and the transition across the threshold is driven by changes in the unstable orbit dynamics induced by the particle parameters. The background-controlled and probe-dynamics-controlled routes therefore represent two realizations of a unified horizon--orbit scale competition rather than independent mechanisms. Among the particle parameters considered, the total angular momentum plays the leading role in controlling the local radial instability, while the spin and charge further modify the instability strength and shift the threshold for chaos bound violation.
