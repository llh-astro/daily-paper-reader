## 摘要
β Pictoris 碎片盘是一个独特系统，其中 JWST/MIRI 的中红外科冕仪成像揭示了一个新的空间分辨子结构，称为猫尾。我们展示了 β Pic 在散射光中的深度 HST/STIS 日冕仪成像，结合了 2012 年至 2025 年间六个历元的观测，以寻找猫尾的散射光成分。我们首次用 HST/STIS 探测到投影分离达 500 au 的盘，在 50 至 200 au 之间的大部分中平面上实现了 SNR > 100。我们报告探测到猫尾子结构的可见光/近红外散射光成分。通过注入-回收分析，我们发现猫尾的散射光成分的总流量为 MIRI F1550C 流量的 0.1–0.4%。利用测得的 STIS 与 MIRI 流量比，我们对猫尾的颗粒性质进行建模，发现这些颗粒高度多孔，且几乎完全由有机难熔材料组成，这与 JWST/MIRI 的发现一致。我们使用太阳系矮行星和本地星际介质中尘埃的有机物含量作为代理，来估计能够产生猫尾中所见足够有机难熔材料的碰撞母体的大小。我们发现每个碰撞母体的质量必须至少为 1–3 × 10^21 kg，与柯伊伯带中的冥卫一（Charon）和鸟神星（Makemake）相当。此外，我们在 β Pic 的外部尘埃晕中发现了显著的翘曲形态和表面亮度不对称。我们将观测到的晕形态和不对称性与预测的垂直结构进行比较，这些结构可能源自行星-盘相互作用。

## Abstract
The $β$ Pictoris debris disk is a unique system where mid-infrared coronagraphic imaging with JWST/MIRI revealed a new spatially resolved substructure known as the Cat's Tail. We present deep HST/STIS coronagraphic imaging of $β$ Pic in scattered light, combining six epochs of observations between 2012 and 2025, in search of the scattered light component of the Cat's Tail. We detect the disk out to a projected separation of 500 au for the first time with HST/STIS, achieving SNR > 100 across much of the midplane between 50 and 200 au. We report the detection of the visible/near-infrared scattered light component of the Cat's Tail substructure. We find through injection-recovery analysis that the scattered light component of the Cat's Tail has a total flux of $0.1-0.4\%$ of the MIRI F1550C flux. Using the measured STIS-to-MIRI flux ratio, we model the Cat's Tail grain properties and find that the grains are highly porous and almost entirely composed of organic refractory material, which is in agreement with findings with JWST/MIRI. We use the organics content of Solar System dwarf planets and dust in the local ISM as a proxy to estimate the size of the colliding progenitors that can produce enough organic refractory material seen in the Cat's Tail. We find that each colliding progenitor must have a mass of at least $1-3\times 10^{21}$ kg, comparable to Charon and Makemake in our Kuiper Belt. Additionally, we find a prominent warped morphology and surface brightness asymmetry in the outer dust halo of $β$ Pic. We compare the observed halo morphology and asymmetry to predicted vertical structures, which may arise from planet--disk interactions.
