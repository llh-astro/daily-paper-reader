## 摘要
最近，在银河系中发现了一群自由漂浮行星。本文考察了被困于原恒星氢云中的行星对气体中引力不稳定性过程的影响。一颗质量足够大的行星可以成为收缩云团的另一个动力学中心。这是由于云内物质吸积到行星上使其质量快速增长所致。由于云中残余的速度梯度和密度梯度，触发恒星形成的行星很可能最终停留在新生恒星周围的遥远轨道上。银河系中的一些第二代及后续代恒星可能通过这一机制形成。

## Abstract
Recently, a population of free-floating planets has been discovered in the Galaxy. This paper examines the influence of the planets trapped within a protostellar hydrogen clouds on the processes of gravitational instability in the gas. A sufficiently massive planet can become an alternative dynamic center for the contracting cloud. This occurs due to rapid mass growth from accretion onto the planet within the cloud. Owing to residual velocity and density gradients in the cloud, the planet that triggers star formation is likely to end up in a distant orbit around the newborn star. Some second- and subsequent-generation stars in the Galaxy may have formed via this mechanism.
