## 摘要
如今已充分确立，经典统计力学源于量子混沌系统的纠缠结构，这由（子系统的）本征态热化假说（ETH）所量化。尽管这一论断基于对此类系统本征态已被充分研究的二分纠缠，近年来研究表明，多体态往往以多分纠缠为特征，这就提出了一个问题：混沌本征态的普适多分纠缠特征是什么？我们通过两类三方纠缠探测量——Rényi 三方多熵与 Rényi 反射熵——研究遍历二分拟设的三方推广，从而部分回答了该问题。我们利用鞍点分析推导了这两个量系综平均的领头阶解析表达式。除这些度量外，我们还得到了 Rényi-2 反射密度矩阵 $ρ_{AA^*}^{(2)}$ 的平均，该矩阵控制 Rényi-2 反射熵。我们表明，混沌本征态中的 $ρ_{AA^*}^{(2)}$ 可由该系综平均很好地描述；与普通约化密度矩阵不同，它在子系统尺寸区间上呈现三种定性不同的形式，这或可视为子系统 ETH 的三方推广。我们通过混合场 Ising 模型的精确对角化来检验我们的解析预测。

## Abstract
It is now well-established that classical statistical mechanics emerges from the entanglement structure of quantum chaotic systems, as quantified by the (subsystem) eigenstate thermalization hypothesis (ETH). While this statement rests on the well-studied bipartite entanglement of the eigenstates of such systems, recent years have shown that many-body states are often characterized by multipartite entanglement, raising the question: what are the universal multipartite entanglement features of chaotic eigenstates? We answer this question in part by studying a tripartite generalization of the ergodic bipartition ansatz through the lens of two tripartite entanglement probes, the Rényi tripartite multi-entropy and the Rényi reflected entropy. We derive leading-order analytical expressions for the ensemble averages of both quantities using a saddle-point analysis. Beyond these measures, we obtain the average of the Rényi-2 reflected density matrix, $ρ_{AA^*}^{(2)}$,which controls the Rényi-2 reflected entropy. We show that $ρ_{AA^*}^{(2)}$ in a chaotic eigenstate is well-described by this ensemble average which, unlike an ordinary reduced density matrix, takes three qualitatively distinct forms across subsystem-size regimes, which may be viewed as a tripartite generalization of subsystem ETH. We benchmark our analytical predictions against exact diagonalization of the mixed-field Ising model.
