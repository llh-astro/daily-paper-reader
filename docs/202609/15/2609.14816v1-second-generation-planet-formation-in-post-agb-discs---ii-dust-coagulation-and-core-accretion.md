## 摘要
观测已证实，围绕后渐近巨星支（post-AGB）双星形成的环双星盘与原恒星周围的原行星盘具有惊人的相似性。在本研究中，我们考察了在后AGB盘中，行星形成核心吸积框架下不同生长阶段的可行性，以评估这些系统中行星形成是否可能。我们发现，在后AGB盘的寿命内，尘埃凝聚至毫米尺度是可能的，这与观测一致。随后，我们研究了星子通过卵石吸积和星子吸积实现的后续生长。如果这些盘中发生流不稳定性，则在质量较高的后AGB盘（$M_{\rm disc}\sim0.1M_\odot$）中形成的星子能够进入快速卵石吸积，并在估计的盘寿命内达到其孤立质量。相比之下，在质量较低的后AGB盘（$M_{\rm disc}\sim0.01M_\odot$）中，星子吸积效率太低，无法产生显著的进一步生长。我们还表明，与原恒星周围的原行星盘相比，后AGB盘具有更大的纵横比，这意味着较高的卵石孤立质量，因此有可能形成质量可达木星质量级别的岩质行星。在评估了这些盘中的气体吸积和同时发生的迁移之后，我们得出结论：只要能够实现局部尘埃与气体比的增强以及足够高的盘质量，后AGB盘中的第二代行星形成在其估计寿命（$10^4$--$10^5$年）内在理论上是可能的。

## Abstract
Observations have established that the circumbinary discs formed around post-asymptotic giant branch (post-AGB) binaries have striking similarities to protoplanetary discs around young stars. In this study, we examine the feasibility of the different stages of growth in the core accretion framework of planet formation in post-AGB discs, in order to assess whether planet formation is possible in these systems. We find that dust coagulation up to mm sizes is possible within the lifetimes of post-AGB discs, consistent with observations. We then investigate the subsequent growth of planetesimals through pebble and planetesimal accretion. If the streaming instability operates in these discs, the resulting planetesimals formed in higher-mass post-AGB discs ($M_{\rm disc}\sim0.1M_\odot$) can enter rapid pebble accretion and reach their isolation mass within the estimated disc lifetime. By contrast, in lower-mass post-AGB discs ($M_{\rm disc}\sim0.01M_\odot$), planetesimal accretion is too inefficient to produce substantial further growth. We also show that the larger aspect ratios of post-AGB discs compared to protoplanetary discs around young stars imply high pebble isolation masses and therefore potential formation of rocky planets with masses extending up to Jupiter masses. After assessing gas accretion and concurrent migration in these discs, we conclude that second generation planet formation in post-AGB discs is theoretically possible within their estimated lifetimes ($10^4$--$10^5$yr) provided that local dust-to-gas ratio enhancements and sufficiently high disc masses are achieved.

## 2. 方法论
- **核心思想**：沿用 PPD 中发展的核心吸积理论，但代入 post-AGB 盘物理条件，逐阶段估算生长效率与时间尺度，并与盘寿命比较。

- **星子形成阶段**：
  - 假设流不稳定性（SI）可在局部尘气比增强时触发，使尘埃团块引力坍缩。

  - 高 \(\alpha\) 时迁移更明显，最终质量更大。

  - 在局部尘气比增强且盘质量足够高（约 \(0.1M_\odot\)）的条件下，
