## 摘要
许多复杂系统仅能通过时间上非配对的分布快照被观测，这使得在缺乏额外假设的情况下，基于轨迹的动力学学习变得困难。因此，我们直接在分布空间中构建该问题，将分布本身视为动力学状态。挑战在于分布空间是无限维的，这使得从有限快照中学习紧致且近似封闭的表示变得困难。我们提出DisKO，通过联合学习具有预测性的分布可观测量、有限维Koopman表示以及回到完整分布的生成映射，将深度Koopman学习扩展到分布动力学。在七个不同的基准任务上，DisKO取得了最先进的外推性能，并在长时程预测任务中误差累积显著更慢。DisKO还在具有解析谱的系统上恢复了主要的Koopman特征值和特征函数，揭示了所学表示中有意义的动力学结构。

## Abstract
Many complex systems are observed only through temporally unpaired distribution snapshots, making trajectory-based dynamical learning difficult without additional assumptions. We therefore formulate the problem directly in distribution space, treating the distribution itself as the dynamical state. The challenge is that distribution space is infinite-dimensional, making compact and approximately closed representations difficult to learn from finite snapshots. We introduce DisKO, which extends deep Koopman learning to distribution dynamics by jointly learning predictive distributional observables, a finite-dimensional Koopman representation, and a generative map back to the full distribution. Across seven diverse benchmarks, DisKO achieves state-of-the-art extrapolation performance, with substantially slower error accumulation on long-horizon prediction tasks. DisKO further recovers leading Koopman eigenvalues and eigenfunctions on systems with analytic spectra, revealing meaningful dynamical structure in the learned representation.
