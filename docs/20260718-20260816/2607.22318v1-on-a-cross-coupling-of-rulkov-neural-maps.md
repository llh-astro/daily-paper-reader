## 摘要
我们引入了一种Rulkov神经映射的新型耦合，并提出了对作用于慢变量的扰动转变为非小值的启发式生物学解释。我们解析地证明了，如果耦合与原始系统相关联，则它保持运动的有界性以及快返斥子（通过Marotto定理导致Devaney混沌）的存在性。对于两个标准混沌Rulkov映射的耦合，我们对系统轨道进行了数值模拟，显示了全局奇异吸引子的出现，其分形结构通过非整数Kaplan-Yorke维数的计算得到了强有力的证明。此外，我们进行了涉及时间序列、Lyapunov指数谱、分岔图和吸引盆的标准数值研究。最后，我们简要提出了将该耦合推广到任意数量神经元的方法。

## Abstract
We introduce a novel coupling of Rulkov neural maps, proposing a heuristic biological interpretation for the transition to non-small values of the perturbations acting on the slow variables. We analytically prove that the coupling preserves boundedness of motion and the existence of a snap-back repeller (leading to Devaney chaos by the Marotto theorem), if they are associated to the original system. For the coupling of two standard chaotic Rulkov maps, we present numerical simulations for the orbits of the system showing the arising of a global strange attractor, whose fractal structure is strongly suggested by the computation of a non-integer Kaplan-Yorke dimension. Furthermore, we perform standard numerical studies concerning time series, Lyapunov exponents spectra, bifurcation diagrams and basins of attraction. Finally, we briefly propose a generalization of the coupling to an arbitrary number of neurons.
