## 摘要
Thornton等人（2026年）从TESS食双星计时中的超额拱线运动报告了27颗非凌星环双星行星候选体。经典恒星贡献对目标特定的半径、拱线常数$k_2$和自转敏感。我们重新评估了该样本中的两颗演化型双谱食双星：脉动系统CH Ind和Am星系统SW CMa。其恒星质量与半径已在专项双星研究中被测定，从而可以直接计算恒星贡献。对于CH Ind，六个独立的TESS扇区节点给出$\dotω_{\rm obs}=1.82^{+0.99}_{-0.94}\times10^{-3}\ {\rm deg\,cycle^{-1}}$，而对所测双星分量的同年龄拟合预测$\dotω_{\rm stars}=2.05^{+0.43}_{-0.34}\times10^{-3}\ {\rm deg\,cycle^{-1}}$。对于SW CMa，其测得尺寸将经典项提高了约16倍；已发表的特定目标预测值$0.670\pm0.020\times10^{-3}\ {\rm deg\,cycle^{-1}}$与观测值$0.690\pm0.050\times10^{-3}\ {\rm deg\,cycle^{-1}}$一致。两个系统均未显示出显著的顺行额外分量。对比表明，来自批量搜索的候选体在赋予第三体解释之前需要目标特定的双星模型。

## Abstract
Thornton et al. (2026) reported 27 non-transiting circumbinary-planet candidates from excess apsidal motion in TESS eclipsing-binary timings. The classical stellar contribution is sensitive to target-specific radii, apsidal constants $k_2$, and rotation. We re-evaluate two evolved, double-lined eclipsing binaries in that sample: the pulsating system CH Ind and the Am system SW CMa. Their component masses and radii have been measured in dedicated binary studies, allowing the stellar contribution to be calculated directly. For CH Ind, six independent TESS sector nodes give $\dotω_{\rm obs}=1.82^{+0.99}_{-0.94}\times10^{-3}\ {\rm deg\,cycle^{-1}}$, while a coeval fit to the measured binary components predicts $\dotω_{\rm stars}=2.05^{+0.43}_{-0.34}\times10^{-3}\ {\rm deg\,cycle^{-1}}$. For SW CMa, its measured dimensions raise the classical term by a factor of about 16; the published target-specific prediction, $0.670\pm0.020\times10^{-3}\ {\rm deg\,cycle^{-1}}$, agrees with the observed $0.690\pm0.050\times10^{-3}\ {\rm deg\,cycle^{-1}}$. Neither system shows a significant excess prograde component. The comparison shows that candidates from a bulk search require target-specific binary models before a third-body interpretation is assigned.
