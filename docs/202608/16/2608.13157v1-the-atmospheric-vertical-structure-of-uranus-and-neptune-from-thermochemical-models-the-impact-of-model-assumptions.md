## 摘要
天王星和海王星大气的成分和温度-压力剖面尚未被很好地确定。由于观测数据有限，我们常常依赖化学平衡计算来推断大气丰度和云的形成。然而，由此推断的大气结构强烈依赖于一些基本假设，例如元素丰度及其比例、假定物质的凝结特性，或绝热结构的参考温度。在本研究中，我们探讨了不同金属丰度（1至80倍太阳丰度）、元素比例（C/O和S/N，分别为0.1至2和0.19至1.6）以及1巴温度（66至86 K）对冰巨行星大气垂直结构的影响。具体而言，我们使用化学平衡代码FastChem推导CH4、NH3、H2S、H2O和NH4SH的混合比和云结构。我们发现模型对假设参数非常敏感，可能产生截然不同的大气结构。对于本文考虑的情况，我们发现混合比和云层高度可能有超过一个数量级的变化。此外，由于成分和1巴温度的不同，热力廓线可能相差数十开尔文。我们建议，未来需要地基观测和专门的天王星和/或海王星探测任务，以更好地表征冰巨行星的大气结构和成分。

## Abstract
The composition and temperature-pressure profile of the atmospheres of Uranus and Neptune are not well-determined. As observational data are limited, we often rely on chemical equilibrium computations to infer atmospheric abundances and cloud formation. The inferred atmospheric structures, however, strongly depend on several fundamental assumptions such as the elemental abundances and ratios, the condensation properties of the assumed species, or a reference temperature for the adiabatic structure. In this study we investigate the effects of different metallicities (1 to 80 solar), element ratios (C/O and S/N, from 0.1 to 2 and 0.19 to 1.6) and 1 bar temperatures (66 to 86 K) on the vertical structure of ice giant atmospheres. In particular, we use the chemical equilibrium code \texttt{FastChem} to derive mixing ratios and cloud structures for CH$_4$, NH$_3$, H$_2$S, H$_2$O and NH$_4$SH. We find that the models are very sensitive to the assumed parameters, yielding drastically different possible atmospheric structures. For the cases considered here, we find that mixing ratios and cloud deck altitudes can vary by more than an order of magnitude. Additionally, thermal profiles can differ by several tens of kelvins due to composition and 1-bar temperature. We advise that future ground-based observations and a dedicated mission to Uranus and/or Neptune are required to better characterize the atmospheric structure and composition of ice giants.
