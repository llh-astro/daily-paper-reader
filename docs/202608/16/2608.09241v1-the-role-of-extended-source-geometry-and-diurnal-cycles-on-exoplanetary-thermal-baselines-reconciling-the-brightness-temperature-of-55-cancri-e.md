## 摘要
本研究在基于几何的InstellCa代码基础上进行了扩展，该代码计算精确的辐照度值，形成了InstellCa-2.0，后者计算给定纬度上行星经度平均的辐照度及其热剖面。辐照度的估算将行星视为一个三维体，相对于恒星旋转，且恒星的天顶角在昼夜循环中变化。我们关注辐照几何对一类可能呈现非同步旋转的超短周期（USP）岩石行星的影响。这作为一个专门案例研究，展示旋转和与主星的距离如何影响裸露岩石世界的估计亮温。选择55 Cancri e专门为了展示此效应的重要性，因为该行星此前被假设存在非同步旋转，且关于其是否存在大气层存在科学争议。结果表明，在裸露岩石行星和相应Bond反照率（A_B = 0.3）假设下，通过几何模型计算的半球平均亮温与JWST MIRI对55 Cancri e（1796 K）的高精度亮温估计极为吻合。这自然为观测到的热特征提供了解释，优雅地调和了先前关于该行星的研究。

## Abstract
This study augments the geometry-based InstellCa code that calculates accurate irradiance values to InstellCa-2.0, which computes the longitude averaged instellation over a given latitude on the planet along with its thermal profile. The estimation of instellation is performed considering the planet as a 3D body that rotates with respect to the star with a varying zenith angle of the star across the diurnal cycle. We focus on the implications of irradiation geometry on a specific class of ultra-short period (USP) rocky planets that may exhibit asynchronous rotation. This serves as a specialised case study to demonstrate how rotation and proximity to host-stars can affect the estimated brightness temperatures of bare rocky worlds. 55 Cancri e is selected specifically to demonstrate the importance of this effect as it has been earlier hypothesised to exhibit asynchronous rotation and also has a debated scientific discourse about the existence of an atmosphere on the planet. The results show excellent agreement of the hemisphere-averaged brightness temperature calculated through the geometric model, under the bare rocky planet and the corresponding Bond albedo (A_B = 0.3) assumption, with the highly precise JWST MIRI brightness temperature estimate for 55 Cancri e (1796 K). This naturally offers an explanation of the observed thermal imprint that elegantly reconciles the previous works on the planet.

### 6. 论文的主要结论与发现

- 在**裸岩无大气 + 非同步旋转 + Bond 反照率 0.3** 的假设下，InstellCa-2.0 计算出的 55 Cancri e 半球平均亮温为 **1797 K**，与 JWST MIRI 观测值 **1796 ± 88 K** 几乎完全一致。
- 这意味着：**不需要引入温室大气**也能解释 55 Cancri e 的热辐射观测，此前基于厚大气的假说并非唯一可行解释。
- 几何扩展源效应导致**极区/半影区永久受照**，形成非零基线温度，其贡献在近距行星中不可忽略。
- 在 TRAPPIST-1 系统中，模型无需传统热重分布因子 f 即可复现观测，说明昼夜循环修正因子 γ 与热重分布因子 f 之间存在**简并性**——几何效应可能被错误归因于大气热输运。
- 该方法提供了一条减少热输运模型简并性的新途径：通过精确辐照几何分析热相曲线。
