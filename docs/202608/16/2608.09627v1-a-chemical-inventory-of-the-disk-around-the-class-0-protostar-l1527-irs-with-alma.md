## 摘要
行星形成始于仍嵌入其诞生包层中的盘。在此，我们汇编了Class 0原恒星L1527 IRS周围盘和包层（<3500 au）化学成分的广泛清单。利用所有公开的ALMA（阿塔卡马大型毫米/亚毫米波阵列）数据，我们报告了39种分子（包括同位素体）的探测。其中，22种为不同分子种类，28种为首次在ALMA观测中朝向L1527报告。CH$_3$OH是探测到的唯一复杂有机分子，而碳氢化合物CH$_3$CCH是探测到的最大分子。总体而言，仅有少数项目足够灵敏，能够基于运动学明确探测到源自盘的发射。含氮分子主要在大尺度上被探测到，而碳氢化合物显示出明显的尾部，大致沿东南外流腔壁分布，这可能是由于东向外流瓣中更强的紫外场。L1527 IRS原恒星系统在含硫分子方面并不丰富，仅观测到CS和SO的强发射。总体而言，包层似乎以富碳化学为主，在盘中似乎转变为富氧化学。我们计算了所有探测到的物种的柱密度，为量化年轻盘之间的化学多样性以及行星形成物质的化学演化提供了起点。

## Abstract
Planet formation starts in disks that are still embedded within their natal envelopes. Here, we compile an extensive inventory of the chemical composition of the disk and envelope ($<$ 3500 au) around the Class 0 protostar L1527 IRS. Using all publicly available ALMA (Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array) data, we report the detection of 39 molecules, including isotopologues. Of these, 22 are different molecular species and 28 are reported here for the first time toward L1527 in ALMA observations. CH$_3$OH is the only complex organic molecule detected, while the hydrocarbon CH$_3$CCH is the largest molecule detected. Overall, only a few programs are sensitive enough to detect emission unambiguously originating from the disk based on the kinematics. Nitrogen-bearing molecules are predominantly detected on more extended scales, while hydrocarbons show a distinct tail roughly along the southeastern outflow cavity wall, probably due to a stronger UV field in the eastern outflow lobe. The L1527 IRS protostellar system is not rich in sulfur-bearing molecules, with only strong emission observed for CS and SO. Overall, the envelope appears dominated by a carbon-rich chemistry, which seems to transition into an oxygen-rich chemistry in the disk. We calculate column densities of all detected species, providing a starting point to quantify the chemical diversity among young disks and the chemical evolution of the planet-forming material.
