## 摘要
由微引力透镜和直接成像调查推断出的自由漂浮行星的普遍性表明，行星抛射——一种最初诞生于恒星宿主周围的行星变得引力不受束缚的过程——是常见的。已提出了四种主要机制来诱发行星抛射：邻近行星的近距离接近、双星或多星系统中的不稳定性、恒星和行星的飞掠，以及主序后恒星演化。在此，我们回顾了行星抛射的潜在机制以及从每种机制中得出的预测。当前和即将进行的微引力透镜调查有望检验现有模型并区分潜在的行星抛射机制，进一步深入了解系外行星在不同恒星环境中的总体架构。

## Abstract
The ubiquity of free-floating planets inferred from microlensing and direct imaging surveys suggests that planetary ejection---a process in which planets initially born encircling a stellar host become gravitationally unbound---is common. Four overarching mechanisms have been proposed to induce planetary ejection: close approaches of neighboring planets, instabilities in binary or multi-star systems, stellar and planetary flybys, and post-main-sequence stellar evolution. Here we review the mechanisms underlying planetary ejection, as well as predictions derived from each. Current and upcoming microlensing surveys offer the potential to test existing models and distinguish between potential planetary ejection mechanisms, offering further insight into the demographic-level architectures of exoplanets across stellar environments.
