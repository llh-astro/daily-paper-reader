## 摘要
白矮星WD J091405.30+191412.25的光谱显示出挥发性物质氢、氧和硫的吸收线和双峰发射线。这一独特特征被解释为这颗白矮星从一个星周盘吸积质量的证据，该盘是由一颗类似海王星或超级蓬松质量的近距离行星上蒸发的大气物质形成的。然而，迄今为止，行星的轨道间距及其质量损失率仅通过简单的解析近似进行了估算。我们使用三维辐射-流体动力学模拟辐照氢大气，并结合一维黏性盘演化模型来研究这一情景。我们计算了在不同轨道间距下，暴露于白矮星极端紫外（XUV）辐射下的类海王星和超级蓬松行星的大气逃逸，并追踪逃逸气体在形成星周盘后的演化。模拟得出的行星质量损失率为$(1.8-4)	imes10^{12}$ g/s。注入的物质形成一个气态盘，通过连续质量供应与吸积到白矮星上的黏性吸积之间的平衡，在不到$10^5$的时间内达到准稳态。由此产生的吸积率与观测估计一致。与之前的解释相反，我们的模型预测盘延伸至行星轨道之外。我们得出结论，一颗位于15个太阳半径处、富含气体的行星经历持续的光蒸发，并自然产生一个星周盘，能够重现WD J0914+1914的观测吸积率和光谱特征。这些结果为蒸发行星情景提供了强有力的支持，并为星周盘的结构和范围提供了新的约束。

## Abstract
The spectrum of the white dwarf WD J091405.30+191412.25 displays the absorption and double-peaked emission lines of the volatiles hydrogen, oxygen, and sulfur. This unique characteristic has been interpreted as evidence of this white dwarf accreting mass from a circumstellar disk that had formed from atmospheric material evaporating off a close-in Neptune-like or super-puff mass planet. Thus far, however, the orbital separation of the planet and its mass-loss rate have only been estimated using simple analytical approximations. We investigate this scenario using 3D radiative-hydrodynamic simulations of irradiated hydrogen atmospheres together with 1D viscous disk evolution models. We compute atmospheric escape from Neptune-like and super-puff planets exposed to extreme ultraviolet (XUV) radiation of the white dwarf at different orbital separations and follow the evolution of the escaping gas after it forms a circumstellar disk. The simulations yield planetary mass-loss rates of $(1.8-4)x10^{12}$ g/s. The injected material forms a gaseous disk that reaches a quasi-steady state in less than $10^5$ through the balance between continuous mass supply and viscous accretion onto the white dwarf. The resulting accretion rates are consistent with observational estimates. In contrast to previous interpretations, our models predict that the disk extends beyond the planetary orbit. We conclude that a gas-rich planet orbiting at 15 solar radii undergoes sustained photoevaporation and naturally produces a circumstellar disk capable of reproducing the observed accretion rates and spectral signatures of WD J0914+1914. These results provide strong support for the evaporating-planet scenario and offer new constraints on the structure and extent of the circumstellar disk.
