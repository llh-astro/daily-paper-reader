## 摘要
非晶固态水上的结合能调控分子云和原行星盘中的分子脱附，但基于第一性原理计算无序冰表面异质结合位点的结合能在计算上是不可行的。我们旨在开发一种快速、可解释的方法来预测非晶固态水上的结合能，该方法无需显式电子结构计算即可捕获表面异质性。我们利用局部冰-吸附剂环境的27个几何描述符训练了一个机器学习模型。该模型在13种吸附质于非晶固态水团簇上的结合能上训练，并通过跨15个冰表面的汇集留一簇交叉验证进行评估。其可迁移性在完全排除于训练之外的自由基物种上进行了评估。该模型在15个冰表面上实现了汇集的留一簇交叉验证决定系数$R^2 = 0.90$（模型捕获的结合能方差比例，其中1为完美拟合），平均绝对误差为378 K。该模型也能迁移到训练中未包含的自由基物种，汇集$R^2 = 0.79$，但当未配对电子直接参与表面相互作用时，其准确性下降。我们的结果展示了一条通向气体-尘埃天体化学模型中快速、分布感知的结合能及脱附/扩散速率参数化的途径，同时保持了在异质冰表面上的物理可解释性。

## Abstract
Binding energies on amorphous solid water regulate molecular desorption in molecular clouds and protoplanetary disks, but computing them from first principles across the heterogeneous binding sites of disordered ice surfaces is computationally prohibitive. We aim to develop a rapid, interpretable method for predicting binding energies on amorphous solid water that captures surface heterogeneity without requiring explicit electronic-structure calculations. We trained a machine-learning model using 27 geometric descriptors of the local ice-adsorbate environment. The model was trained on binding energies for 13 adsorbates on amorphous solid water clusters and evaluated through pooled leave-one-cluster-out validation across 15 ice surfaces. Its transferability was assessed on radical species excluded entirely from training. The model achieves a pooled leave-one-cluster-out coefficient of determination $R^2 = 0.90$ (the fraction of variance in the binding energies captured by the model, where 1 is a perfect fit), and a mean absolute error of 378 K across 15 ice surfaces. It also transfers to radical species withheld from training, with a pooled $R^2 = 0.79$, although its accuracy decreases when the unpaired electron contributes directly to the surface interaction. Our results demonstrate a route towards rapid, distribution-aware parameterisation of binding energies and desorption/diffusion rates in gas-grain astrochemical models, while preserving physical interpretability on heterogeneous ice surfaces.
