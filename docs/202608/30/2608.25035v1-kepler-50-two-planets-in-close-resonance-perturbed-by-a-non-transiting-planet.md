## 摘要
Kepler-50 拥有两颗接近 6:5 可公度性的凌星行星。我们利用动力学拟合分析了从 Kepler 数据中测得的凌星时间。我们将残余信号归因于系统中一颗近共振的非凌星第三行星。第三行星的参数存在简并。我们为外（内）摄动体确定了 55（15）个感兴趣区域，并通过后验采样刻画了每个感兴趣区域中的系统参数。我们发现，剔除不长期稳定的样本对后验几乎没有额外约束，并且凌星时间不能证实凌星行星可能的二体共振状态，因为约 68% 可能稳定的后验样本也可能处于天平动中。我们识别出一小部分处于三体天平动的样本。尽管存在简并，我们恢复了 Kepler-50 b 和 c 的稳健且约束良好的质量（分别为 2.35^{+0.48}_{-0.43} M⊕ 和 4.43^{+0.45}_{-0.49} M⊕），并强烈探测到 Kepler-50 b 和 c 的非零相对偏心率：(e_c cos ϖ_c - e_b cos ϖ_b) = -0.045 ± 0.003，(e_c sin ϖ_c - e_b sin ϖ_b) = -0.030 ± 0.004。这对邻近行星分别位于半径谷和亚海王星尺寸范围内（1.750 ± 0.038 R⊕ 和 2.079 ± 0.060 R⊕）。作为已知最近的行星对之一，且最接近一阶可公度性的系统之一，Kepler-50 系统是行星形成理论特别有价值的基准。

## Abstract
Kepler-50 has two transiting planets close to the 6:5 commensurability. We analyze transit times measured from Kepler data with dynamical fits. We attribute the residual signal to a near-resonance non-transiting third planet in the system. The parameters of the third planet are degenerate. We identified 55 (15) regions of interest for an exterior (interior) perturber, and characterize system parameters in each region of interest with posterior sampling. We find that removing samples that are not long-term stable provides few additional constraints on posteriors, and that the transit times do not confirm the likely 2-body resonant state of the transiting planets as $\approx$ 68\% of posterior samples that are likely stable are also likely in libration. We identify a small fraction of samples that are in 3-body libration. Despite the degeneracies, we recover robust, tightly constrained masses for Kepler-50 b and c ($2.35^{+0.48}_{-0.43}$ $M_{\oplus}$ and $4.43^{+0.45}_{-0.49}$ $M_{\oplus}$, respectively) and strongly detected non-zero relative eccentricity for Kepler-50 b and c with ($e_c\cos\varpi_c$ - $e_b\cos\varpi_b$) = -0.045 $\pm 0.003$, and ($e_c\sin\varpi_c$ - $e_b\sin\varpi_b$) = -0.030 $\pm 0.004$. These close neighbors are in the radius valley and sub-Neptune by size, respectively ($1.750 \pm 0.038$ $R_{\oplus}$ and $2.079 \pm 0.060$ $R_{\oplus}$). With one of the closest planet pairs known and one of the closest to first-order commensurability, the Kepler-50 system is an especially valuable benchmark for planet formation theory.

## 8. 不足与局限

- **第三行星参数仍高度简并**：Kepler-50x 的质量、周期、相位无法唯一确定，也无法排除其他更复杂的构型（如多个非凌星行星）。
