## 摘要
围绕M矮星运行的潮汐锁定系外行星是搜寻宜居世界的首要目标，然而其复杂的气候动力学对传统的宜居性模型构成了挑战。我们对具有最高最新地球相似指数的系外行星进行了同质的液态水宜居性估计。我们修改了气候模型PLASIM，将其应用于一系列参数范围内的潮汐锁定系外行星。我们系统地分类并比较了潜在宜居的系外行星，并从宜居世界目录的保守样本中选取了地球相似指数最高的那些，既进行了彼此之间的比较，也作为大气压力的函数进行了比较。我们的分析揭示了三种主要气候状态：雪球、热和眼球行星——后者具有温暖的、宜居的星下点区域（T > 273 K）和局部水文循环。尽管表面温度通常随压力升高而升高，但眼球行星的气候在较宽的压力范围内保持稳定。复杂的大气动力学控制着气候的行为，凸显了进行详细气候模拟以指导未来观测的必要性。

## Abstract
Tidally locked exoplanets orbiting M dwarf stars are prime targets in the search for habitable worlds, yet their complex climate dynamics challenge conventional models of habitability. We homogeneously estimated the liquid-water habitability of exoplanets with the highest up-to-date Earth-similarity index. We modified the climate model PLASIM to apply it to tidally locked exoplanets across a range of parameters. We systematically classified and compared potentially habitable exoplanets and selected among the ones featuring the highest Earth-similarity index in the conservative sample of the Habitable Wolds Catalog, both relative to each other and as a function of atmospheric pressure. Our analysis revealed three main climate regimes: Snowball, Hot, and Eyeball planets-the latter having a warm and habitable substellar region with T > 273 K and a localized hydrological cycle. Although surface temperature generally increases with pressure, Eyeball planet climates remain stable in a wide pressure range. Complex atmospheric dynamics govern the behavior of the climate, highlighting the need for detailed climate simulations to guide future observations.
