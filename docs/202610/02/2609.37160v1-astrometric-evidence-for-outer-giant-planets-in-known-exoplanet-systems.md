## 摘要
外层巨行星会影响其行星系统的形成与动力学演化。然而，它们的长轨道周期使其难以通过凌星和径向速度（RV）技术探测。在Gaia DR4历元天体测量数据发布之前，来自Hipparcos、Gaia DR2和DR3（G23H）的测量可以探测由远距伴星引起的恒星反射运动。利用G23H星表，我们在已知的凌星和RV系统中识别出29个外层伴星候选体（$a_{median}$ > 8.4 AU）。从170个具有$P(\mathrm{companion})>0.75$且对$0.3$–$13\,M_{\rm J}$伴星有后验支持的系外行星宿主星出发，我们保留了29个系统；在这些系统中，每个被选中的候选伴星的中位半长轴都超出最外层已知行星，并且每颗已知行星在质量和半长轴上都位于该候选伴星95\%最高后验密度区域之外。我们结合文献中已发表的RV和成像数据，进一步约束外层候选体的质量和半长轴。在样本中的八个系统里，文献已经报道了RV变化或趋势。所报道的候选体有可能解释这些RV变化，但需要进行天体测量与RV联合拟合，以确定是否是同一些伴星产生了两种信号。这些结果为进一步的RV监测以及与Gaia DR4的联合轨道分析提供了一组高优先级目标。如果这些伴星得到确认，其轨道约束将为研究它们对已知内层行星动力学演化的影响提供一个起点。

## Abstract
Outer giant planets can influence the formation and dynamical evolution of their planetary systems. However, their long orbital periods make them difficult to detect using transit and radial velocity (RV) techniques. Measurements from Hipparcos, Gaia DR2 and DR3 (G23H) can detect the stellar reflex motion induced by distant companions before the release of Gaia DR4 epoch astrometry. Using the G23H catalogue, we identify 29 outer companion candidates ($a_{median}$ $>$ 8.4 AU) in known transiting and RV systems. Starting from 170 exoplanet hosts with $P(\mathrm{companion})>0.75$ and posterior support for $0.3$--$13\,M_{\rm J}$ companions, we retain 29 systems where each selected component has a median semi-major axis beyond the outermost known planet with every known planet outside its 95\% highest posterior density region in mass and semi-major axis. We incorporate published RV and imaging data from the literature to further constrain the outer candidate's mass and semi-major axis. In eight systems present in the sample, an RV variation or trend has already been reported in the literature. The reported candidates could potentially explain these RV variations, but joint astrometric and RV fits are needed to establish whether the same companions produce both signals. These results provide a set of high priority targets for further RV monitoring and joint orbit analysis with Gaia DR4. If these companions are confirmed, their orbital constraints will provide a starting point for studies of their influence on the dynamical evolution of the known inner planets.
