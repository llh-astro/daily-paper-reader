## 摘要
星子是行星形成的关键中间阶段，起源于原行星盘中的尘埃聚集，而这一过程受到气体动力学的强烈调控。在本研究中，我们使用 Athena++ 的多流体尘埃模块，研究原行星盘外部区域的二维轴对称全局非理想磁流体力学（MHD；双极扩散，AD）模拟中的尘埃聚集。我们的模拟同时激发大规模 MHD 盘风，并解析流动不稳定性（SI）和垂直剪切不稳定性（VSI）。我们系统探究了磁场强度、冷却时标、双极埃尔萨瑟数以及尘埃尺寸对尘埃聚集的影响。我们发现，在广泛的磁化和发射盘风条件下，强烈的尘埃结块持续存在。MHD 盘风还通过以下方式改变背景条件来增强尘埃结块：（1）MHD 盘风质量损失导致的长期气体耗竭；（2）磁通量集中（出现在磁化更强的情况中）导致尘埃在纬向流中捕获。总体而言，我们的结果表明，在具有 AD 和大规模 MHD 盘风的全局磁化盘中，强烈的尘埃结块能够持续存在，从而形成有利于星子形成的条件。

## Abstract
Planetesimals are a key intermediate stage of planet formation and originate from dust concentration in protoplanetary disks, which is strongly regulated by gas dynamics. In this study, we use the multifluid dust module of Athena++ to investigate dust concentration in 2D axisymmetric global non-ideal MHD (ambipolar diffusion, AD) simulations of outer disks. Our simulations simultaneously launch large-scale MHD disk winds and resolve both streaming instability (SI) and vertical shear instability (VSI). We systematically explore the effects of magnetic field strength, cooling timescale, ambipolar Elsasser number, and dust size on dust concentration. We find that strong dust clumping persists across a broad range of magnetized and wind-launching disk conditions. MHD winds also enhance dust clumping by modifying background conditions in the following ways: (1) secular gas depletion by MHD wind mass loss, and (2) dust trapping in zonal flows as a result of magnetic flux concentration (which occurs in more strongly magnetized cases). Overall, our results demonstrate that strong dust clumping can persist in global magnetized disks with AD and large-scale MHD winds, leading to conditions favorable for planetesimal formation.
