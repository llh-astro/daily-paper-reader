## 摘要
受跨海王星天体（TNO）Máni 的奇特特征以及半人马族天体与跨海王星天体周围环系统发现的启发，我们研究了粒子在一个拥有深赤道撞击坑的小型不规则天体周围的稳定性。本研究是致力于非球对称天体周围动力学的系列工作的第三篇。我们以 Máni 作为参考模型，其撞击坑深度超过其半径的 10%，并通过互补方法绘制系统稳定性：庞加莱截面（PSS）、存活图以及有限时间李雅普诺夫指数（FTLE）。在名义情形下，1:1、2:1、3:1 和 4:1 自旋-轨道共振（SORs）被所有技术一致识别。显著的 3:1 SOR 在高偏心率下表现出分岔结构，与 PSS 中较低雅可比常数值处观测到的结构转变相匹配。增大旋转速率（λ）和撞击坑质量比（μ）会扩大共振宽度并促进重叠，使 2:1 和 4:1 SOR 逐渐占主导。较低的旋转速率允许稳定轨道在更高偏心率下持续存在，而 1:1 SOR 对更强扰动高度敏感，并在最极端情形下消失。与可能清空 2:1 SOR 内侧区域的质量异常模型相比，这里考虑的撞击坑构型在该区域附近保留了稳定区域，这为不规则小天体周围碎片结构的形成与维持提供了替代情景。

## Abstract
Motivated by the peculiar features of the trans-Neptunian object (TNO) Máni and the discovery of ring systems around Centaurs and trans-Neptunian bodies, we investigate the stability of particles around a small irregular body hosting a deep equatorial crater. This study is the third contribution in a sequence devoted to the dynamics around non-spherically symmetric bodies. Using Máni as a reference model, whose crater depth exceeds 10% of its radius, we map the system stability through complementary methods: Poincaré surfaces of section (PSS), survival maps, and the finite-time Lyapunov exponent (FTLE). In the nominal case, the $1\!:\!1$, $2\!:\!1$, $3\!:\!1$, and $4\!:\!1$ spin-orbit resonances (SORs) are consistently identified by all techniques. The prominent $3\!:\!1$ SOR exhibits a bifurcated structure at high eccentricities, matching structural transitions observed at lower Jacobi constant values in the PSSs. Increasing the rotation rate ($λ$) and crater mass ratio ($μ$) enlarges resonance widths and promotes overlap, making the $2\!:\!1$ and $4\!:\!1$ SORs progressively dominant. Lower rotation rates allow stable trajectories to persist at higher eccentricities, whereas the $1\!:\!1$ SOR is highly sensitive to stronger perturbations and disappears in the most extreme cases. In contrast to mass-anomaly models, which can clear the region interior to the $2\!:\!1$ SOR, the crater configuration considered here preserves stable regions in this vicinity, suggesting alternative scenarios for the formation and maintenance of debris structures around irregular minor bodies.
