## 摘要
目标。本研究利用基于模拟光变曲线的自回归行星搜索（ARPS）方法，考察 PLATO 任务对小型凌星行星（半径在 0.5 至 2.0 R⊕ 之间）的探测灵敏度。方法。我们检验了数千条类 PLATO 光变曲线的模拟，这些模拟来自 PLATO 南天长观测阶段 2（LOPS2）天区预期的磁活跃恒星，并注入了半径小于 2.0 R⊕ 的凌星行星。使用 ARPS 分析这些光变曲线；ARPS 采用自回归建模滤除非行星信号，并使用 Transit Comb Filter 周期图进行行星凌星探测。通过对两个 ARPS 参数——周期图峰值功率和凌星持续时间——设置阈值，减少了不存在的周期信号引起的误报。结果。当应用于 PLATO 对亮星（P1 和 P2 样本）模拟的前三个月观测时，对于周期短于 40 d 的地球大小行星，至少 50% 能够被恢复。在 LOPS2 的两年观测中，对于周期小于 240 d 的行星，25% 能够被恢复。讨论了该方法在去趋势、周期图、误报、审查和星表评估方面的方法学启示。展示了探测与审查后阳性预测值热图的效用。结论：虽然 ARPS 行星探测流程并不完全适合 PLATO 分析，但本研究为小型凌星行星探测的灵敏度和可靠性提供了方法学见解。

## Abstract
AIM. This study examines the PLATO mission sensitivity to small transiting planets (radii between 0.5 and 2.0 R$_\oplus$) using Autoregressive Planet Search (ARPS) methodology based on simulated lightcurves.   Methods. Several thousand simulations of PLATO-like lightcurves from magnetically active stars expected from the PLATO Long Observation Phase South 2 (LOPS2) field with injected transiting planets smaller than 2.0 R$_\oplus$ radius are examined. The lightcurves are analyzed with ARPS that uses autoregressive modeling for filtering of nonplanetary signals and the Transit Comb Filter periodogram for planetary transit detection. False alarms of nonexistent periodic signals are reduced by thresholds on two ARPS parameters, peak periodogram power, and transit duration.   Results. When applied to PLATO's simulated first three months observations of bright stars (P1 and P2 samples), at least 50% of Earth-size planets are recovered for periods shorter than 40 d. In two years of LOPS2 observation, 25% are recovered for periods less than 240 d. Methodological implications on detrending, periodograms, False Alarms, vetting, and catalog evaluation are discussed. The utility of a positive predicted value heat map after detection and vetting is demonstrated.   Conclusions: While the ARPS planet detection procedure is not entirely well-suited for PLATO analysis, this study provides methodological insight into the sensitivity and reliability of small transiting planet detection.
