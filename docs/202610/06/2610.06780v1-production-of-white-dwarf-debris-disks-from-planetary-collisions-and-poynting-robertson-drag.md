## 摘要
本文探讨了白矮星周围碎屑盘的产生，观测表明这些白矮星会经历频繁的污染事件。在该情景中，行星碰撞发生在一颗高度演化的 AGB 星表面之外不远处，此时该恒星已结束质量损失阶段，并开始向白矮星过渡。碰撞使一部分行星物质汽化，随后这些物质膨胀、冷却、成核为液滴，并凝结成特征尺度约为 $s\sim1$ cm 的岩石天体。岩石碎屑盘在由恒星光度驱动的 Poynting-Robertson 拖曳作用下演化；该光度起初很大，但随时间稳步下降。这一情景为将岩石物质输送到白矮星紧邻区域提供了另一种通道。

## Abstract
This paper explores the production of debris disks around white dwarfs, which are observed to experience frequent pollution events. In this scenario, planetary collisions occur just outside the surface of a highly evolved AGB star, after the end of its mass loss phase, as it starts its transition into a white dwarf. The collisions vaporize a fraction of the planetary material, which subsequently expands, cools, nucleates droplets, and condenses into rocky bodies with characteristic size of order $s\sim1$ cm. The rocky debris disk evolves through the action of Poynting-Robertson drag, driven by the stellar luminosity, which is initially large but steadily decreases with time. This scenario provides an alternate channel for delivering rocky material to the immediate vicinity of a white dwarf.
