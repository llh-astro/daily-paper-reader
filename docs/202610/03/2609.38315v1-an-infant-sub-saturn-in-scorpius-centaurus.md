## 摘要
我们报告了 TIC 88297141 Ab 的发现与验证，这是一颗凌星行星，围绕天蝎-半人马 OB 星协中一颗前主序 M 型矮星（0.31 太阳质量，0.74 太阳半径）运行。其星群成员身份表明年龄为 $15.6 \pm 1.6$ Myr，并得到锂和等时线定年的支持。我们使用为变星设计的自定义管线探测到 TESS 凌星信号，并利用地基测光（LCO/Swope 和 LCOGT/Sinistro）确认该信号来自目标天体。我们对凌星和恒星活动进行全局拟合，得到轨道周期为 4.64 天，行星半径为 $7.21 \pm 0.32$ 地球半径（0.64 木星半径）。该恒星有一颗共动 M 型矮星伴星（0.4 太阳半径），投影距离为 $260$ au；散斑成像（Gemini-S/Zorro）未揭示其他恒星伴星，而 Magellan/PFS 的径向速度与恒星活动一致，在 $2σ$ 下给出 $M_p \sin i < 4.7$ 个木星质量。统计验证分析给出 $10^{-5}$ 的假阳性概率，从而验证了该行星。质量测量将确定 TIC 88297141 Ab 是一颗低密度超级蓬松行星——类似这些年龄上其他大小相近的行星——还是一颗相对于其半径质量异常大的行星。

## Abstract
We report the discovery and validation of TIC 88297141 Ab, a transiting planet orbiting a pre-main-sequence M dwarf (0.31 solar masses, 0.74 solar radii) in the Scorpius-Centaurus OB association. Group membership implies an age of $15.6 \pm 1.6$ Myr, supported by lithium and isochrone age-dating. We detected the TESS transits using a custom pipeline designed for variable stars and used ground-based photometry (LCO/Swope and LCOGT/Sinistro) to confirm the on-target nature of the signal. Our global fit of the transits and stellar activity gives an orbital period of 4.64 days and a planet radius of $7.21 \pm 0.32$ Earth radii (0.64 Jupiter radii). The star has a comoving M-dwarf companion (0.4 solar radii) at a projected separation of $260$ au; speckle imaging (Gemini-S/Zorro) reveals no additional stellar companions, and radial velocities from Magellan/PFS are consistent with stellar activity, giving $M_p \sin i < 4.7$ Jupiter masses at $2σ$. A statistical validation analysis yields a false positive probability of $10^{-5}$, validating the planet. A mass measurement will determine whether TIC 88297141 Ab is a low-density super-puff, like other comparably sized planets at these ages, or an unusually massive planet for its radius.
