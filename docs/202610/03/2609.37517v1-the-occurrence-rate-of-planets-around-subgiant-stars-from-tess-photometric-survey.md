## 摘要
亚巨星是恒星演化中的一个关键阶段，为研究行星系统如何响应后主序膨胀的早期阶段提供了机会。尽管其重要性，与主序星和红巨星宿主相比，针对这一群体的系统性出现率研究仍然稀少。在本工作中，我们对从Gaia DR3星表选出的282,904颗亚巨星样本进行了行星出现率的统一分析。我们对TESS光变曲线进行独立再分析，以检测和精化凌星信号，随后进行多阶段审查，包括目视检查和统计假阳性评估。我们通过注入-回收实验量化巡天的探测灵敏度，在恒星样本中注入了565,808个合成凌星信号。在1–10天的周期范围以及探测完备性得到良好表征的行星半径范围内，我们测得完备性校正后的行星出现率为0.644^{+0.048}_{-0.041}。出现率强烈依赖于行星半径，其中1–4 R_earth范围内的行星贡献最高，并在12–15 R_earth处存在次级增强。这些结果为研究经历后主序早期演化的恒星周围的近距行星族群提供了族群统计基线。

## Abstract
Subgiant stars represent a critical phase in stellar evolution, offering an opportunity to investigate how planetary systems respond to the early stages of post-main-sequence expansion. Despite their importance, systematic occurrence-rate studies of this population remain sparse compared with those of main-sequence and red-giant hosts. In this work, we present a uniform analysis of planet occurrence around a sample of 282,904 subgiant stars selected from the Gaia DR3 catalogue. We perform an independent reanalysis of TESS light curves for transit-signal detection and refinement, followed by multi-stage vetting including visual inspection and statistical false-positive assessment. We quantify the detection sensitivity of the survey through injection-and-recovery experiments, with 565,808 synthetic transit signals injected across the stellar sample. Over the period range of 1--10 days and the planet-radius range for which the detection completeness is well characterised, we measure a completeness-corrected planet occurrence rate of 0.644^{+0.048}_{-0.041}. The occurrence rate is strongly dependent on planet radius, with the highest occurrence contributed by planets in the 1-4 R_earth range and a secondary enhancement at 12-15 R_earth. These results provide a demographic baseline for studying the population of close-in planets around stars undergoing early post-main-sequence evolution.
