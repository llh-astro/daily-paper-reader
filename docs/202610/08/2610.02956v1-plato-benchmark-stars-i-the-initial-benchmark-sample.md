## 摘要
欧洲空间局（ESA）的PLATO任务旨在利用高精度测光与星震学，探测并表征环绕明亮晚型恒星运行的地球大小系外行星。宿主恒星的精确恒星参数对于系外行星表征和模型基准测试至关重要。本工作定义了PLATO基准星（PBS）星表，这是一个经过充分表征的恒星综合样本，旨在支持任务的所有阶段。我们汇编了来自食双星和分光双星、干涉测量、星震学以及大型光谱和测光巡天的数据。对恒星基本参数和大气参数（质量、半径、光度、年龄、有效温度、表面重力、密度和临边昏暗）赋予了质量等级（B1、B2、B3），以反映其模型依赖程度。还提供了均一化的辅助参数（包括Gaia DR3偏差校正视差、金属丰度、热辐射通量、消光、自转周期，以及M矮星的X射线通量）。PBS星表包含1090颗FGKM主序和亚巨星，依据模型无关（B1）参数划分为三个质量层级：金（15颗具有B1质量、半径和光度）、银（195颗具有B1质量和半径）和铜（416颗至少具有一项B1属性）。其余恒星则具有充分表征的模型依赖（B2）或经验（B3）属性。约40%的样本位于PLATO视场内（LOPN1中285颗，LOPS2中143颗）。PBS样本是迄今汇编的最大基准星星表，为验证自动化流程、检验演化模型和跨巡天校准提供了关键资源。该星表可在线访问 https://astro.ulb.ac.be/pbs，并将定期更新。

## Abstract
The ESA PLATO mission aims to detect and characterise Earth-size exoplanets orbiting bright, late-type stars using high-precision photometry and asteroseismology. Precise stellar parameters of host stars are essential for exoplanet characterisation and model benchmarking. This work defines the PLATO benchmark stars (PBS) catalogue, a comprehensive sample of well-characterised stars designed to support all mission phases. We compiled data from eclipsing and spectroscopic binaries, interferometry, asteroseismology, and large spectroscopic and photometric surveys. Fundamental and atmospheric stellar properties (mass, radius, luminosity, age, effective temperature, surface gravity, density, and limb darkening) were assigned quality levels (B1, B2, B3) reflecting their degree of model dependence. Homogenized ancillary parameters (including Gaia DR3 bias-corrected parallaxes, metallicities, bolometric fluxes, extinction, rotation periods, and X-ray fluxes for M dwarfs) are also provided. The PBS catalogue contains 1090 FGKM main-sequence and subgiant stars, divided into three quality tiers based on model-independent (B1) parameters: gold (15 stars with B1 mass, radius, and luminosity), silver (195 with B1 mass and radius), and bronze (416 with at least one B1 property). The remaining stars feature well-characterised model-dependent (B2) or empirical (B3) properties. About 40% of the sample lies within the PLATO fields of view (285 in LOPN1 and 143 in LOPS2). The PBS sample represents the largest catalogue of benchmark stars assembled to date, providing a key resource for validating automated pipelines, testing evolutionary models, and cross-survey calibration. The catalogue is available online at https://astro.ulb.ac.be/pbs and will be updated regularly.
