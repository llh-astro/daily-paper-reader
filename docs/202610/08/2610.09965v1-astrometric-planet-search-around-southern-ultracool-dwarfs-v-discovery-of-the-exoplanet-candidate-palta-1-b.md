## 摘要
我们报告了 PALTA-1 b 的发现，这是一个亚木星质量的系外行星候选体，围绕距离约17 pc的邻近早L型矮星 DENIS-P J1253108-570924（DE1253-57）运行。该系外行星是通过使用甚大望远镜上的 FORS2 对这颗L矮星的天体测量运动进行超过八年的监测，并刻画其相对于预期单源运动的偏离而发现的。估计的行星质量介于0.54 MJup和0.77 MJup之间，取决于宿主超冷矮星质量的不确定性。该系统质量比约为0.010，相对间距估计为1.6–3.4 au。如果得到确认，PALTA-1 b 将成为已知极少数围绕邻近超冷矮星运行的巨行星实例之一。这一发现最终完成了一项更大的天体测量行星搜寻巡天，该巡天以20颗邻近超冷矮星为目标，并此前发现了三个新的超冷双星系统。

## Abstract
We report the discovery of PALTA-1 b, a sub-Jupiter mass exoplanet candidate orbiting the nearby early-L dwarf DENIS-P J1253108-570924 (DE1253-57) located at a distance of ~17 pc. The exoplanet was discovered by monitoring the L dwarf's astrometric motion with FORS2 at the Very Large Telescope over more than eight years and characterising the deviations from the expected single-source motion. The estimated planet mass is between 0.54 MJup and 0.77 MJup, depending on the uncertain mass of the ultracool dwarf host. The system's mass ratio is about 0.010, and the relative separation is estimated at 1.6-3.4 au. If confirmed, PALTA-1 b would be one of the very few known examples of a giant planet orbiting a nearby ultracool dwarf. This discovery finalises a larger astrometric planet search survey that targeted 20 nearby ultracool dwarfs and previously discovered three new ultracool binary systems.
