## 摘要
近距离轨道上的小型系外行星受到高水平的恒星辐照，这可通过大气逃逸有效剥离低质量的原始H/He包层。在此条件下，那些质量与半径既不符合裸岩质内部、也不符合延展的以H为主的大气的行星，预计会拥有高平均分子量的大气，这使其成为富挥发分热的水世界的有前景候选体。我们首次对低密度超级地球TOI-2211 b（P~3.09 d）进行了详细表征，这是一颗最近得到验证的、环绕一颗K型恒星（G星等：9.1）的凌星行星。结合TESS和CHEOPS测光数据以及来自HARPS、ESPRESSO和KPF的径向速度测量，我们将该行星的半径和质量精确约束为 $1.457 \pm 0.047$ R$_\oplus$ 和 $2.69 \pm 0.56$ M$_\oplus$。我们还在径向速度数据中识别出一颗外侧的非凌星行星候选体（P~15.6 d，Msin(i) $ = 8.53 \pm 1.04$ M$_\oplus$）。我们研究了这颗凌星行星的内部结构，并通过详细的蒸发分析评估了推断出的挥发分包层的稳定性，从而将TOI-2211 b认定为有前景的热的水世界候选体。因此，TOI-2211 b加入了这类近距离低密度超级地球的规模虽小但不断增长的样本，它们为检验当前行星形成与演化理论提供了特别有趣的目标。此外，我们使用行星形成与演化的生成模型研究了该行星系统的完整构型，所得后验分布显示出与径向速度数据中识别出的非凌星行星候选体的观测轨道周期和最小质量一致的明显过密度。

## Abstract
Small exoplanets on close-in orbits are exposed to high levels of stellar irradiation, which can effectively remove low-mass primordial H/He envelopes through atmospheric escape. Under these conditions, planets whose masses and radii are incompatible with both bare rocky interiors and extended H-dominated atmospheres are therefore expected to host high mean molecular weight atmospheres, making them promising candidates for volatile-rich hot water worlds. We present the first detailed characterisation of the low-density super-Earth TOI-2211 b (P~3.09 d), a recently validated transiting planet orbiting a K type star (G mag: 9.1). Combining TESS and CHEOPS photometry with radial velocity measurements from HARPS, ESPRESSO, and KPF, we precisely constrain the planet's radius and mass to $1.457 \pm 0.047$ R$_\oplus$ and $2.69 \pm 0.56$ M$_\oplus$. We also identify an outer, non-transiting planetary candidate (P~15.6 d, Msin(i) $ = 8.53 \pm 1.04$ M$_\oplus$) in the radial velocity data. We investigate the internal structure of the transiting planet and assess the stability of the inferred volatile envelopes through a detailed evaporation analysis, thereby identifying TOI-2211 b as a promising hot water world candidate. TOI-2211 b therefore joins the small but growing sample of these close-in low-density super-Earths, which represent particularly interesting targets for testing current planet formation and evolution theories. Additionally, we investigate the full architecture of the planetary system using a generative model of planet formation and evolution, with the resulting posterior distributions showing a clear over-density consistent with the observed orbital period and minimal mass of the non-transiting planetary candidate identified in the radial velocity data.
