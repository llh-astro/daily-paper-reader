## 摘要
刻画恒星形成早期阶段形成的复杂有机分子的清单，仍是天体化学中的一个基本研究问题，这些分子可能影响原行星盘乃至行星的最终化学组成。阿塔卡马大型毫米波/亚毫米波阵列（ALMA）提供的角分辨率和灵敏度使其能够聚焦观测深嵌的太阳型原恒星周围的热（T > 100 K）气体，并高精度地绘制其分子清单。ALMA大型计划“利用ALMA光谱巡天观测原恒星中的复杂有机分子”（COMPASS）旨在提供一组原恒星源分子组成的全面清单，以理解其环境和演化阶段的化学影响。COMPASS 是一项对 11 个谱线丰富的 0 类和 I 类原恒星源的无偏光谱巡天，使用 ALMA 在 279.0 至 311.7 GHz 的光谱范围内以 0.15–0.5″ 的角分辨率进行，对应于目标源周围约 100 au（半径）的内部区域。本文概述了 COMPASS 计划以及所采用的策略，包括观测设置和目标源。我们还对选定分子的空间分布以及选定复杂有机物的谱线流量相对强度进行了定性比较。定性比较表明，源之间可能存在化学差异，例如含氧物种组与含氮物种组之间的丰度差异，但也表明此类变化小于一个数量级。在这些水平上进行丰度的定量比较，需要巡天的大带宽，以及对巡天中每个源探测到的数千条谱线的激发和强度进行仔细分析，这将在后续论文中探讨。

## Abstract
It remains a fundamental research question in astrochemistry to characterize the inventory of complex organic molecules formed during the early stages of star formation, which may affect the eventual chemical composition of protoplanetary disks and potentially planets. The Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array (ALMA) provides the angular resolution and sensitivity to zoom in on the hot (T > 100 K) gas surrounding embedded solar-type protostars and chart their molecular inventories with high accuracy. The ALMA Large Program Complex Organic Molecules in Protostars with ALMA Spectral Surveys (COMPASS) aims to provide comprehensive inventories of the molecular content of a sample of protostellar sources to understand the chemical impact of their environments and evolutionary stages. COMPASS is an unbiased spectral survey of 11 line-rich Class 0 and I protostellar sources in the spectral range from 279.0 to 311.7 GHz at 0.15-0.5" angular resolution with ALMA, corresponding to the inner ~100 au (radius) around the targeted sources. This paper provides an overview of the COMPASS program and the adopted strategy in terms of observational setups and targeted sources. We also present a qualitative comparison of the spatial distributions of selected molecules and the relative strengths of the line fluxes of selected complex organics. The qualitative comparisons suggest that chemical differences between sources may be present, for example, between the abundances of groups of oxygen- and nitrogen-bearing species, but also that such variations occur at levels smaller than an order of magnitude. Quantitative comparisons of abundances at these levels require the large bandwidth of the survey, as well as a careful analysis of the excitation and strengths of the several thousands of lines detected toward each source in the survey, as will be explored in forthcoming papers.
