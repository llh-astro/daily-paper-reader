## 摘要
太阳系类似的气态巨行星和宜居带类地行星对于传统的系外行星探测与表征技术（如凌星法、径向速度法和直接成像法）而言难以观测。跨多个孔径的长基线消零干涉测量可以抑制星光，并在比单孔径望远镜上的传统日冕仪更高的空间分辨率下探测微弱的行星信号。借助电信行业的技术进步，光子集成电路（PIC）为执行天文应用所需的光学操作提供了一个有前景的平台，包括长基线和交叉孔径配置中用于消零干涉测量的定相和光束组合。与分立光学元件相比，PIC提供了紧凑、可扩展的结构，对对准以及热和机械扰动的敏感性更低。然而，其设计和制造精度仍不足以满足系外行星仪器的严格要求。在此，我们研究了光子三耦合器的消零能力，这种器件由三个等宽波导组成，其对称结构使光束组合后天生具备产生消色差零点的倾向。在实验室中，我们在平面二氧化硅-硅平台上使用1.55 μm单色光表征了器件的消零深度。在宽带H波段光中，我们探讨了来自用于精细定相的热光相位调制器等组件的色散效应。我们致力于推进PIC用于系外行星直接探测和大气表征的成熟化，这将支持未来天基天文台（如宜居世界天文台）高对比度技术的可扩展测试和部署。

## Abstract
Solar System analog gas giants and habitable-zone terrestrial planets are observationally elusive to conventional exoplanet detection and characterization techniques, i.e. transits, radial velocities, and direct imaging. Long baseline nulling interferometry across multiple apertures suppresses starlight and enables detection of faint planetary signals at higher spatial resolution than traditional coronagraphs on single-aperture telescopes. Leveraging technological advancements from the telecommunications industry, photonic integrated circuits (PICs) offer a promising platform for performing the optical operations necessary for astronomical applications, including phasing and beam combination for nulling interferometry in both long-baseline and cross-aperture configurations. PICs provide compact, scalable architectures with reduced sensitivity to alignment as well as thermal and mechanical perturbations compared to bulk optics. However, their design and manufacturing precision remain insufficient for the stringent requirements of exoplanet instrumentation. Here, we investigate the nulling capabilities of photonic tricouplers, devices composed of three equal-width waveguides that are geometrically predisposed to produce achromatic nulls upon beam combination through their symmetric construction. In the laboratory, we characterize null depths in monochromatic light at 1.55 $\mathrmμ$m with devices on a planar silica-on-silicon platform. In broadband $H$-band light, we explore chromatic effects from components such as thermo-optic phase modulators used for fine-phasing. Our ongoing efforts towards maturation of PICs for direct detection and atmospheric characterization of exoplanets will support scalable testing and deployment of high-contrast technologies for future space-based observatories, such as the Habitable Worlds Observatory.
