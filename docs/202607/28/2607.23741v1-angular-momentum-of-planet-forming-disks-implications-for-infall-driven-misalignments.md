## 摘要
背景。超过30%的行星形成盘和行星系统与其宿主恒星的旋转轴存在错位，但导致这些错位的主要机制仍不清楚。目标。我们旨在通过观测限制Class II原行星盘的角动量，并评估物质的晚期内落是否能带来足够的角动量使其倾斜。方法。我们首先计算了15个盘的角动量，这些盘的表面密度分布来自对高角分辨率ALMA观测的动力学建模。基于该样本，我们推导出盘的角动量与恒星质量、盘质量以及包围90% 13CO通量的半径之间的关系，并用其估计了另外18个盘的角动量。然后，我们将盘的值与来自云和观测到的流星的晚期吸积的理论预测进行比较。结果。大多数盘的角动量低于理论模型对晚期内落的预测。这与流星的观测比较也定性一致，然而需要表征供给流星的储库质量来确认这一图像。结论。与附近云的相互作用导致物质晚期内落到Class II盘上，可以潜在地解释盘内和行星系统内观测到的错位。

## Abstract
Context. A significant fraction (>30%) of planet-forming disks and planetary are misaligned with respect to the rotational axis of their host stars, yet the dominant mechanism responsible for these misalignments remains unclear. Aims. We aim to observationally constrain the angular momentum of Class II protoplanetary disks and assess whether late-stage infall of material can bring sufficient angular momentum to tilt them. Methods. We first computed the angular momenta of 15 disks with surface density profiles inferred from dynamical modeling of high angular resolution ALMA observations. Based on this sample, we derived a relation linking disk angular momentum to stellar mass, disk mass, and the radius enclosing 90% of the 13CO flux and used it to estimate angular momenta of 18 more disks. We then compared disk values with theoretical predictions for late-stage accretion from clouds and observed streamers. Results. Angular momentum for most disks is lower than what theoretical models predict for late infall. This is also in qualitative agreement with comparison with streamer observations, however, characterization of mass of reservoirs feeding the streamers is needed to confirm this picture. Conclusions. Interactions with nearby clouds, resulting in late-stage infall of material onto Class II disks, can potentially explain the observed misalignments within disks and planetary systems.
