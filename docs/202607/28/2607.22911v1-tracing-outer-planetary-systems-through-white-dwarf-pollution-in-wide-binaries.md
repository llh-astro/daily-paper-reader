## 摘要
白矮星大气中的金属污染为残余行星物质的存在提供了有力证据，并可被用作外行星系统的间接示踪剂。我们通过比较宽双星中氢大气白矮星与视作单星的白矮星中钙（Ca）污染的 incidence，分析宽伴星的存在是否会影响外行星系统的出现。我们构建了两组样本对：一组是较大的同质SDSS低分辨率光谱样本，另一组是较小的高分辨率样本。对于每个白矮星，我们测量钙丰度或上限，并计算可探测性校正后的累积污染分数，该分数考虑了有效温度、信噪比和光谱分辨率的变化。在基于SDSS的样本中，我们发现在单白矮星和宽双星中的白矮星中，污染分数<1%（定义为检测到钙的白矮星的比例）。在可探测性校正后，两个群体的累积丰度分布在统计上是一致的。对于高分辨率样本，尽管它们的原始检测分数不同，也得到了相同的结论。我们的结果表明，在当前不确定性范围内，单星和具有宽（~200 AU）伴星的恒星之间残余外行星系统的出现率没有统计上的显著差异。这些不确定性很大，因为样本中只包含少量被污染的白矮星。这里使用的可探测性校正累积分数方法为比较具有不同探测灵敏度的样本提供了一个框架，并可扩展到更大的光谱数据集。将需要这样的样本来确定宽伴星是否影响行星物质的存活和输送，并寻找更微妙的趋势，例如，作为伴星分离度的函数。

## Abstract
White dwarf (WD) atmospheric metal pollution provides strong evidence for the presence of remnant planetary material and it can be used as an indirect tracer of outer planetary systems. We analyze whether the presence of a wide stellar companion affects the occurrence of outer planetary systems by comparing the incidence of calcium (Ca) pollution in hydrogen-atmosphere WDs in wide binaries and in apparently single stars. We construct two sample pairs: a large homogeneous SDSS low-resolution spectroscopic set and a smaller high-resolution sample. For each WD, we measure Ca abundances or upper limits and compute detectability-corrected cumulative pollution fractions that account for variations in effective temperature, signal-to-noise ratio, and spectral resolution. In the SDSS-based samples, we find pollution fractions <1%, defined as the fraction of WDs with detected Ca, for both single WDs and WDs in wide binaries. After correcting for detectability, the cumulative abundance distributions of the two populations are statistically consistent. The same conclusion is obtained for the higher-resolution samples, despite their different raw detection fractions. Our results indicate no statistically significant difference in the occurrence rate of remnant outer planetary systems between single stars and stars with wide (~200 au) companions, within the current uncertainties. These uncertainties are significant because the samples contain only a small number of polluted WDs. The corrected detectability cumulative fraction approach used here provides a framework for comparing samples with different detection sensitivities and can be extended to larger spectroscopic datasets. Such samples will be required to determine whether wide stellar companions affect the survival and delivery of planetary material and to search for more subtle trends, for example, as a function of companion separation.
