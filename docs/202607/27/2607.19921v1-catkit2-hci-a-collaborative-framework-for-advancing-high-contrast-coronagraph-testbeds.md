## 摘要
高对比度系外行星成像需要专门的实验室测试床，用于开发和验证日冕仪架构、波前传感与控制方法、校准策略以及系统级观测概念。这些测试床通常有类似的软件需求，但许多工具是在各个机构独立开发的。CATKit2-HCI（高对比度成像协作）通过提供一个共享的软件框架来弥补这一差距，支持可重复使用的高对比度成像基础设施。CATKit2-HCI基于CATKit2构建，CATKit2是一个开源硬件控制与同步框架，最初为太空望远镜科学研究所的复杂孔径望远镜高对比度成像仪（HiCAT）测试床开发。CATKit2-HCI为针对高对比度成像的特定算法、校准工具、诊断、可视化和性能指标提供了协作层。该协作目前包括美国和欧洲的多个日冕仪测试床。其目标是减少重复的软件开发，通过共享代码审查提高代码质量，实现跨设施结果的更直接比较，并促进学生、博士后研究人员和合作者在实验室之间的流动。我们描述了CATKit2-HCI作为加速日冕仪技术发展框架的动机、架构、协作模式、共享技术能力以及早期跨测试床示例。

## Abstract
High-contrast exoplanet imaging requires dedicated laboratory testbeds for the development and validation of coronagraph architectures, wavefront sensing and control methods, calibration strategies, and system-level observing concepts. These testbeds often share similar software needs, yet many tools are developed independently at each institution. The CATKit2-High-Contrast-Imaging collaboration, or CATKit2-HCI, addresses this gap by providing a shared software framework for reusable HCI infrastructure. Built on top of CATKit2, an open-source hardware control and synchronization framework originally developed for the High-contrast Imager for Complex Aperture Telescopes (HiCAT) testbed at the Space Telescope Science Institute, CATKit2-HCI provides the collaborative layer for HCI-specific algorithms, calibration tools, diagnostics, visualization, and performance metrics. The collaboration currently includes multiple coronagraph testbeds in the United States and Europe. Its goals are to reduce duplicated software development, improve code quality through shared review, enable more direct comparison of results across facilities, and facilitate the movement of students, postdoctoral researchers, and collaborators between laboratories. We describe the motivation, architecture, collaboration model, shared technical capabilities, and early cross-testbed examples of CATKit2-HCI as a framework for accelerating coronagraph technology development.
