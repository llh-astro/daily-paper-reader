## 摘要
尘埃粒子在原行星盘中根据其环境经历化学反应，产生行星物质的组成多样性。不可逆反应的地外记录，例如无定形硅酸盐的结晶，对原太阳盘的早期演化提供了特别强的约束。在本研究中，我们使用蒙特卡洛粒子追踪模拟，研究了粘性演化盘中此类不可逆反应及反应尘埃的时空分布。我们将预测不可逆反应有效进行温度（“反应线”）的公式（最初为稳态吸积盘开发）扩展到粘性膨胀盘。反应尘埃的时空分布由反应线和滞止线的相对位置决定，滞止线将盘中的内向和外向平流分开。随着盘冷却，反应线向内移动，而由于盘的径向粘性扩散，滞止线向外移动。当反应线远在滞止线内侧时，反应尘埃保留在反应线内。另一方面，当反应线位于滞止线附近或外侧时，位于滞止线附近或两条线之间的反应尘埃被有效地向外输运。这导致反应尘埃在盘中径向广泛分布，包括温度过低无法发生反应的外部区域。我们评估了与太阳系彗星中观察到的晶质硅酸盐一致的盘条件，发现原太阳盘可能是紧凑的、中等质量且不强烈湍流的。

## Abstract
Dust particles undergo chemical reactions in protoplanetary disks according to their environments, producing compositional diversity in planetary materials. Extraterrestrial records of irreversible reactions, such as crystallization of amorphous silicates, provide particularly strong constraints on the early evolution of the protosolar disk. In this study, we investigate such irreversible reactions and the spatiotemporal distribution of reacted dust in a viscously evolving disk using Monte Carlo particle-tracking simulations. We extend a predictive formula for the temperature at which irreversible reactions proceed efficiently ("reaction line"), originally developed for steady accretion disks, to viscously expanding disks. The spatiotemporal distribution of reacted dust is governed by the relative locations of the reaction line and the stagnation line, which separates inward and outward advection in the disk. The reaction line moves inward as the disk cools, while the stagnation line moves outward owing to the radial viscous spreading of the disk. When the reaction line lies far inside the stagnation line, the reacted dust remains inside the reaction line. On the other hand, when the reaction line lies near or beyond the stagnation line, the reacted dust located near the stagnation line or between the two lines is transported outward efficiently. It results in a radially broad distribution of reacted dust throughout the disk, including the outer regions where the temperatures remain too low for reactions. We assessed the disk conditions consistent with the crystalline silicates observed in Solar System comets and found that the protosolar disk was likely compact, moderately massive, and not strongly turbulent.

### 6. 论文的主要结论与发现

1. **反应线预测公式可扩展到粘性演化盘**：通过使用初始特征吸积率，稳态反应线公式能准确预测演化盘中的反应温度，偏差在可接受范围内。
2. **尘埃分布由反应线与滞止线相对位置决定**：
   - 反应线远在内侧：反应尘埃局限内盘。

3. **强烈湍流盘可出现“化石”反应尘埃**：内盘冷却后反应尘埃被吸积消耗，而早期输运到外盘的尘埃得以保留，导致外盘晶质比例高于内盘。
4. **原太阳盘条件约束**：为解释彗星中10–60%的晶质硅酸盐及橄榄石/辉石比值多样性，原太阳盘应：紧凑（r_d0 < 5 au）、中等质量（M_d0 > 0.05 M☉）、非强湍流（α ~ 10⁻³而非10⁻²）。无需额外输运过程（如冰砾石粘附）即可自然实现。
