## 摘要
系外行星的日冕仪成像受限于模拟行星的残余散斑。先进的后处理对当前和未来的地基或天基仪器至关重要。现有技术耗时且有限。ADI需要长序列且在较小分离距离上受限。RDI也耗时且对散斑演化敏感，导致不完美的减法。我们成功在SPHERE上演示的相干差分成像（CDI）通过利用散斑与行星之间的光非相干性提供了一种更快的替代方案。然而，其对精确仪器模型的依赖限制了其性能。在这项工作中，我们引入了EPICX，一种使用梯度提升决策树的增强型CDI方法。EPICX将差分信号建模为高维回归问题，优化了相干散斑噪声与非相干行星信号之间的区分。我们使用不同日冕仪的模拟数据（包括JWST和Roman上的日冕仪）验证了这种增强型CDI方法。

## Abstract
Coronagraphic imaging of exoplanets is limited by residual speckles that mimic planets. Advanced post-processing is essential for current and future instruments on the ground or in space. Current techniques are time-intensive and limited. ADI requires long sequences and is limited at small separations. RDI is also time-consuming and sensitive to speckle evolution, leading to imperfect subtraction. Coherence Differential Imaging (CDI), which we successfully demonstrated on SPHERE, offers a faster alternative by using the light incoherence between speckles and planets. However, its reliance on accurate instrumental models limits its performance. In this work, we introduce EPICX, an enhanced CDI method using gradient-boosted decision trees. EPICX models the differential signal as a high-dimensional regression problem, optimizing the discrimination between coherent speckle noise and incoherent planet signal. We validate this enhanced CDI method using simulated data for different coronagraphs, including those aboard JWST and Roman.
