## 摘要
许多白矮星受到金属污染，这些金属通常被认为是行星系统吸积后的残留物。对这些系统进行建模时，通常假设金属在白矮星大气中均匀分布。然而，白矮星的磁场可能通过磁层吸积影响吸积几何结构。白矮星光球中的对流随后会将金属输送到表面，其结构由相对下沉与扩散的时间尺度决定。在这项工作中，我们构建了磁白矮星的吸积几何结构、后续扩散以及观测到的污染模型。我们表明，磁场最初会将污染集中到白矮星表面的狭窄区域。相对扩散和下沉的时间尺度决定了金属是均匀分布还是局限于局部斑块。如果磁场与自转极不对齐，那么斑块状白矮星会表现出周期性变化的污染特征，从而可以约束斑块面积。我们探索了这个模型作为最近在磁白矮星中探测到周期性变化污染特征的一种可能解释。最后，我们还证明，由于磁场导致物质集中，可能会系统性地低估这些天体的质量吸积率。

## Abstract
Many white dwarfs are polluted by metals, which are generally understood to be the accreted remnants of a planetary system. Modeling these systems typically assumes that the metal concentration is homogeneous throughout the white dwarf's atmosphere. However, the magnetic fields of a white dwarf may affect the accretion geometry of the white dwarf via magnetospheric accretion. Convection in the white dwarf's photosphere will then transport the metals across the surface, with a structure set by the relative sinking versus spreading timescales. In this work, we construct models for the accretion geometry, subsequent spreading, and observed pollution of magnetic white dwarfs. We show that the magnetic fields will initially concentrate the pollution into a narrow region of the white dwarf's surface. The relative spreading and sinking timescales determine whether the metals become uniformly distributed or remain confined to localized patches. If the magnetic field and spin poles are misaligned, then patchy white dwarfs exhibit periodically variable pollution signatures, which enable constraints on the patch area. We explore this model as a possible explanation for the recent detection of periodically variable pollution signatures in magnetic white dwarfs. Finally, we also demonstrate that the concentration of material due to the magnetic field may lead to systematic underestimates of the mass accretion rate onto these objects.
